EXPERT AI PETRA — IT14.92 CHANGE REPORT
=======================================

PURPOSE
IT14.92 is a non-regression economy-control pass built from the two IT14.91 replay
failures on 2026-09-10. The central bug was architectural: the forecast correctly
reported wood critical while stone/metal were surplus, but many civilians physically
mining those surpluses still carried food_owned/future-farmer metadata. The rebalance
layer therefore failed to see them as donor workers.

FILES CHANGED FROM IT14.91
- petra/expertDecisionController.js
- petra/expertDecision/policy.js
- petra/expertDecision/resourceForecast.js
- EXPERT_BENCHMARKS.txt
- IT14.92_CHANGE_REPORT.txt                  [NEW]

COMBAT FREEZE
- petra/attackManager.js is byte-for-byte unchanged from IT14.91.
- petra/attackPlan.js is byte-for-byte unchanged from IT14.91.
- IT14.90 bounded attack-move and CC capture finish therefore remain untouched.

1) ONE ECONOMIC AUTHORITY AFTER THE PROTECTED OPENING
- After the normal opening/adaptive handoff, the four-resource forecast is the sole
  cross-resource rebalance authority.
- The old food-vs-wood, mining-bootstrap and fixed strategic-metal peelers no longer
  compete with forecast moves after that handoff.
- The proven opening safety nets remain available before the forecast control window.
- A forecast wood shortage can make wood eligible for post-opening flexible civilians
  even when the older food/wood feedback signal has not yet entered wood_release.

2) ACTUAL WORK IS TRUTH
- Added live physical resource detection from the worker's real supply/order/carry state.
- Rebalancing chooses donor workers by what they are actually gathering first, permanent
  metadata second.
- A food_owned/future-farmer civilian physically mining 3k-surplus stone now counts as a
  stone worker and is immediately eligible to leave it for a critical resource.
- Temporary fallback workers are preferred before uprooting genuine specialists.
- If the top surplus has no movable workers, the controller can use the next real
  forecast surplus instead of stalling on an impossible donor.
- Temporary future farmers keep their long-term food/future-farmer ownership when moved
  to an emergency resource; they can still be reclaimed by the next Field crew.

3) FORECAST: LIQUIDITY VS STRATEGIC SUPPLY
- IT14.91 allowed nearby resource stock to make a resource look healthy even when current
  income was nearly zero. IT14.92 separates:
    LIQUIDITY = bank + measured current income
    STRATEGIC SUPPLY = nearby own/neutral stock that still needs workers to gather.
- Worker allocation is driven primarily by liquidity coverage.
- Strategic supply answers whether the shortage can be solved locally or requires a
  dropsite/territorial expansion; it does not erase a zero-income cash-flow problem.
- A large bank still prevents a false crisis from a temporary zero gather rate.
- Forecast diagnostic now reports both liquid and strategic margins:
    [EXPERT-FORECAST] ... W=bank/income/access/demand/L.../S...:status

4) NO-IDLE FALLBACK MAY NOT CREATE USELESS SURPLUS
- Before the adaptive economy starts, opening food/wood workers no longer fail a gather
  order and immediately spill into stone/metal.
- After the handoff, fallback choices are filtered through the forecast.
- Critical/short resources rank first; forecast-surplus resources are rejected as
  fallback work when useful alternatives exist.
- If every fallback resource is already over-covered, brief useful waiting is permitted
  instead of mining another several thousand surplus resources.
- Diagnostic:
    [EXPERT-FORECAST] useful-wait ... reason=no-nonsurplus-fallback

5) FUTURE-FARMER PIPELINE RECOVERY
- IT14.90's core contract remains: post-opening new civilians are reserved for permanent
  farm expansion; normally 3-4 build a Field and those same workers stay farming.
- Farm-labor accounting now counts actual permanent farmers plus uncommitted future
  farmers, not every food_owned civilian who may actually be mining elsewhere.
- If a Field is ready but the explicit future-farmer pool was lost behind older metadata,
  IT14.92 can recover eligible POST-OPENING civilians from temporary/surplus work.
- It never recovers a worker from a resource currently forecast critical/short.
- Under real food pressure, the existing Field-progress watchdog may act after ~6s once
  enough future farmers are ready instead of waiting the full ordinary watchdog delay.
- Diagnostics:
    [EXPERT-FARM-CREW] recover future-farmer=...
    [EXPERT-FARM-CREW] field ... stay=farmer

6) WOOD DISTRICT FOLLOWS THE LIVE CUTTING FRONT
- The current forest is no longer defined only by fixed 30m/52m rings around the old
  Storehouse.
- Expert identifies the trees its committed lumberjacks are actually cutting, expands
  those targets into the nearby practical forest, and carries that live cutting face
  forward as part of the same economic district.
- A worker keeps its existing woodsite assignment while that live cutting front remains
  usable instead of clearing the assignment and marching to a distant forest.
- Before switching an established crew, Storehouse placement can advance along that same
  cutting front when >=8 workers still have >=450 wood and average haul is >=14m.
- The IT14.87 emergency escape remains: a truly dead/stalled forest can still be abandoned.

7) STOREHOUSE BUILDING MUST MATCH LABOR INTENT
- A proactive mixed wood/stone/metal Storehouse with zero current workers is rejected
  unless it is a deliberate forecast-critical prebuild.
- Mixed/mineral Storehouse completion no longer turns its builders into lumberjacks.
  Only a genuine wood Storehouse commits its builders to the woodsite.
- This separates "build a useful dropsite" from "switch the whole lumber economy".
- Opening Storehouse builder commitment remains preserved.
