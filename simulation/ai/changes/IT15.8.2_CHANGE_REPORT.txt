PETRA EXPERT IT15.8.2 — POST-WICKER CAPACITY CORRECTION
======================================================

BASELINE
- Directly built on the corrected IT15.8.1 source.

REPLAY FINDING
- The opening cluster had five live bushes.
- The secondary source was one 400-food apple tree.
- Two new civilians had already been assigned to the apple before Wicker completed.
- The old peel routine then assigned all three surplus opening workers to that branch because it did not subtract the two existing apple workers.

CORRECTION
- Secondary branch open slots are now calculated per live supply using the existing supply limits and current worker loads.
- Apple capacity remains three total civilians.
- In the observed 5-bush + apple case, five remain on berries, one peeled civilian joins the two apple workers, and the other two peeled civilians return to wood.

UNCHANGED
- All IT15.8.1 Field, Farmstead, Barracks, CC-production, economy-safety, and combat corrections.
