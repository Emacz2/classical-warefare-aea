CWA Petra Expert AI — IT15.6 Change Report

Base: IT15.5. IT15.4 remains a failed regression branch.

PRIMARY CHANGES

1. NATURAL FOOD FIRST IS NOW A HARD CONTRACT
- Territory natural-food transition threshold moved to 25%.
- Permanent Field demand cannot grow while healthy owned natural food remains.
- The IT15.2 idle-food watchdog no longer forces Fields from transient idle food labor while natural food is healthy.
- Overflow food civilians remain productive on temporary owned resources and retry food.

2. OPENING ECO TECH ORDER
- Berry-heavy openings treat 4+ fruit supplies / 500+ fruit as enough to prioritize Wicker Baskets.
- Intended sequence: Farmstead -> Wicker Baskets -> Iron Axe -> natural-food depletion -> Fields/Plows.
- Plows is hard-gated behind the real natural-food transition, including the P1 eco sweep.

3. FIRST-HOUSE / FOOD-DISTRICT LAYOUT
- First House no longer uses a food-annex candidate.
- It searches a compact 28-54m CC ring toward the wood economy.
- The selected opening food/berry district is explicitly reserved so House #1 cannot consume the future Farmstead/field footprint.

4. 13-HOUSE CITY-STATE BREAKPOINT
- P2 Forge-Tech and P3 Boom suppress normal House #14/#15 at the 13-house commitment even before Town.
- pop_house_01/Home Garden remains the normal 180-pop route once Town is available.
- P1 all-ins remain free to brute-force houses when timing requires it.

5. FIRST TEMPLE CENTRALIZATION
- Temple #1 is capped at 52m from the CC and prefers ~30m.
- Placement scores total worker aura coverage plus extra value for stone/metal and food/farm workers.
- Later temples retain flexible economic/expansion placement.

6. MAJOR BUILDING CONSTRUCTION TEMPO
- Arsenal/Gymnasium/Prytaneion/Cleruchy use 5 builders normally, 8 with an abundant bank, up to 10 with a severe surplus.
- Nearby wood workers receive first claim so construction does not summon builders across the map.

7. EXPANSION PRODUCTION
- Completed Athens Cleruchies now use continuous current+next queue maintenance, stale-standby pruning, idle-gap diagnostics, and 2-3 unit batches when affordable.

8. FASTER TECH-ORIENTED FORGES
- P2 Forge #1 target reduced from pop90 / 7:00 to pop70 / 5:30 conditions.
- P3 Forge #1 target pop60 / 5:00; Forge #2 pop72; Forge #3 pop90 with six Fields OR healthy-natural infrastructure substitution.
- No military-tech completion gate was relaxed.

PRESERVED FROM IT15.5
- Gaia/wildlife cannot trigger Expert strategic defense.
- Wood migration staging may never intentionally idle a worker.
- IT15.3 Storehouse forecast runtime fix.
- P1 retreat/recovery logic, Barracks 2-3 batching, hard sovereignty, Iphicrates protection, and canonical benchmark methodology.
