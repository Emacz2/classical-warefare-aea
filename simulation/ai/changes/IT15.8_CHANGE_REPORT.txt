CWA Petra Expert AI — IT15.8 Change Report

Base: IT15.7. IT15.8 is an economy-kernel stabilization release. Record-setting P2 combat logic remains frozen.

WHY IT15.8 EXISTS
- IT15.7 exposed an architectural failure: Expert could correctly forecast a wood catastrophe yet trap itself with stronger rules layered above Petra.
- In the failed replay, Expert reached 12,247 food / 2 wood / 7,144 stone / 2,449 metal with zero active woodcutters while still assigning large labor to food/stone.
- `woodCiv` metadata was not enough: nominal lumberjacks could have no real legal wood order, creating NO-TARGET loops and 50-93 repeated recovery failures.
- A second IT15.7 replay survived the same class of wood shortage only because it reached Town, bartered surplus into wood, and unlocked zero-wood Slingers. IT15.8 makes those escape routes deliberate instead of accidental.

ECONOMIC SAFETY KERNEL
1. Economic survival outranks strategy.
   - Critical primary-resource continuity overrides rush labor ownership, worker inertia and ordinary resource preferences.
   - Safety is based on ACTUAL gather orders, not permanent job labels.

2. Earlier wood emergency detection.
   - After the protected opening, critical wood with <=6 actual woodcutters and <=250 wood activates safety before income reaches absolute zero.
   - Existing phase/wood-income stall watchdogs also activate it immediately.
   - Extreme resource imbalance can activate the same path.

3. Repair real lumberjacks before creating more metadata.
   - Workers already labeled `wood` / `citizenSoldierWood` but lacking a live wood gather/return order are repaired first.
   - Then idle workers, stone, metal and surplus food labor are peeled toward wood.
   - Every reassignment must produce a real gather target or the previous job metadata is restored.

4. Absolute wood-access escape valve.
   - Normal Expert gathering remains owned-territory-only.
   - If owned wood is exhausted, or the wood watchdog proves zero real flow despite a stale forecast, a capped 16-worker rescue may use safe Gaia wood on the same land access.
   - Rescue distance affects ranking, never legality: the nearest safe same-land neutral wood is preferable to economic death.
   - Neutral rescue shuts off automatically once the wood reserve reaches ~700 / the emergency clears.

5. Failed P1 releases the economy.
   - Once an aborted Early/Late P1 has no live Rush, doctrine converts immediately to `p2_tech_push` recovery.
   - A historical `expertRushHasLaunched` flag can no longer block the wood-scarcity pivot.
   - Village recovery civilians are capped at 40 (Early) / 42 (Late), not 65. Town may reopen the normal P2 civilian ceiling.

6. No impossible assignment loops.
   - Safety resets stale failed-target state rather than letting a worker retry an impossible wood order dozens of times.
   - No-idle fallback tries the critical resource first, but if access is genuinely unavailable it does not compound failure metadata while the access/barter layer recovers.

7. Stop feeding the surplus during wood collapse.
   - With a healthy food bank, new Field/Farmstead actions are suppressed during an active wood emergency.
   - Existing farms keep gathering; scarce wood is reserved for recovery/phase/housing/market infrastructure.

8. Barter is an immediate Town escape route.
   - Emergency wood barter no longer waits until 9:00. Once a Market exists, <250 wood can immediately convert disposable stone/food/metal toward the existing 700-wood target.

9. Resource-substitution military production.
   - During hard wood safety, infantry selection prefers the lowest-wood affordable unit for every civilization.
   - Athens preferentially uses unlocked zero-wood Slingers and the slinger unlock treats economic safety as explicit wood pressure.

10. Food also has a hard floor.
   - A mature critical-food state with near-zero actual food income can override ordinary job ownership and restore real food gatherers. Natural-food-first / delayed-Field doctrine remains unchanged outside that emergency.

IT15.8 MATURE-POPULATION CALIBRATION (same version, post human-vs-Expert review)
11. Expert gather/trade bonus reduced from +35% to +30%.
    - This intentionally removes part of the artificial economic advantage so future gains must come from better decisions.
    - Historical +35% benchmark results remain historical and are not directly comparable to new +30% tests.

12. Global civilian growth ceiling reduced from 65 to 60.
    - P2 Tech Push and P3 Boom grow to 60 permanent civilians once.
    - Rush soft caps remain lower as before; failed-rush Village recovery remains 40/42 until Town.

13. Reaching 60 is a milestone, not a permanent refill order.
    - Once 60 civilians has been reached once, later civilian casualties are NOT automatically replaced back to 60.
    - The live replacement target becomes the dedicated food-engine requirement: four civilians per completed Field plus civilians currently using natural food, with a small 24-civilian absolute rebuild floor.
    - Example: 10 completed Fields with no natural food -> 40-civilian replacement target. If 60 falls to 50 or 45, the CC trains soldiers instead of civilians. If it falls below 40, civilians are restored only to the food requirement.

14. Mature civilian food backbone.
    - After the 60-civilian milestone, the controller preserves the required number of civilians as the preferred food workforce.
    - If farmer civilians are killed while secondary-resource civilians survive, surviving civilians rotate into food before the CC retrains civilian replacements.
    - Citizen-soldiers increasingly inherit wood/stone/metal work as civilian casualties release population.
    - This turns attrition into a more military population composition instead of mechanically recreating the original worker mix.

FROZEN FOR THIS CALIBRATION
- Combat/attack authorization and execution.
- IT15.8 economic-safety kernel and emergency wood escape.
- Farm timing, Wicker/berry handling, housing, tech timing and construction logic are unchanged in this calibration pass.
