IT14.85 CHANGE REPORT

Focused changes from IT14.84; only expertDecisionController.js and expertDecision/policy.js changed.

1. P3 MAX-POP / SIEGE DEADLOCK
- Population reserve now scales with every missing requested siege engine (2 missing = two reserve pockets, not one).
- Two siege remain preferred.
- At max population, one real siege engine may launch if the opponent is already badly outmatched or the hero+tech package is otherwise complete.
- Absolute deadline can no longer be blocked forever by a missing second engine.

2. P3 RESEARCH UTILIZATION
- Military research remains first priority during City/P3 preparation.
- If no military tech can be queued at that moment, free minor-tech capacity immediately falls through to core eco/mining/surplus economic upgrades.

3. FARM-DISTRICT RESERVATION
- Dedicated permanent Farmstead build actions are placement-resolved before same-frame independent structures.
- A selected Farmstead immediately reserves its future compact Field district before its foundation materializes.
- Temple/Market/Storehouse/etc. therefore cannot steal the 3-4 Field faces that justified the Farmstead site.

4. CITY PREREQUISITE / MARKET #2
- Town-class foundations and active Expert Town-building tasks count toward the projected City requirement.
- A Temple already in the construction pipeline prevents a redundant second Market.
- If Market #2 is genuinely needed, P3 minimum spacing is restored to 70m with a 110m preferred separation.

5. COMPACT, ECO-SAFE HOUSING
- Houses preferentially form small 2-3-house snap blocks using the actual House footprint dimensions.
- Once a compact component reaches 3 Houses, the next House starts a new block instead of extending one endless line.
- Houses remain excluded from existing Storehouse work rings.
- Expert also protects up to four high-value unserved future wood-worksite pads, so housing cannot occupy a location that should become a more efficient Storehouse.
- Existing farm-district/resource-corridor/resource-footprint protections remain active.
