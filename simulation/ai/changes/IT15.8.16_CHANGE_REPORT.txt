IT15.8.16 CHANGE REPORT

BASE
IT15.8.16 is a narrow conversion correction built directly on IT15.8.15.
It preserves the second replay's stronger population growth, opening placements,
20-civilian normal wood ceiling, Town eco research, expansion, and combat changes.

CONTROL COMPARISON — REPLAYS 20260917-135253 / 135259
At 5:00, the second IT15.8.15 replay reached 66 population versus 56 and had
gathered 297 more food plus 804 more wood. It nevertheless held only 71 food
and 823 wood, so the stronger economy still felt slow and starved.

ROOT CAUSES
- Field #1 was requested around 1:20, but the crew filter rejected reserved future
  farmers until roughly 3:10 because it treated every currently-food-classified
  civilian as protected natural-food labor.
- Post-opening civilian batches used artificial 350/700 food thresholds for four/five
  even though civilian cost is 50 food; another batch of three was locked too early.
- Predictive housing continued buying Houses #3/#4 with 10-13 real population slots.
- Six Fields remained the practical one-Barracks stopping point despite food below 200.

CORRECTIONS
1. A future farmer may build a Field while temporarily categorized as food unless it
   carries a real natural-food supply lock. Original fruit workers remain protected.
2. After the stable three-civilian opening, batch size uses actual affordability:
   four at 200 food and five at 250 food, still bounded by cap/headroom.
3. Before 6:00, every House after House #1 waits until actual population has six or
   fewer free slots. A pending House and genuine cap emergency remain authoritative.
4. With one or more Barracks, exhausted natural food, population 50+, and food below
   200, Field #7 becomes an immediate pre-Town food floor.

EXPECTED 1-5 MINUTE EFFECT
- Preserve IT15.8.15's equal 1:00/2:00 population and stronger 4:00/5:00 growth.
- Move the first Field materially earlier than ~3:10 and accelerate Fields 2-6.
- Convert part of the 823-wood 5:00 bank into food capacity rather than extra Houses.
- Produce four/five-civilian batches whenever the real bank supports them.
