PETRA EXPERT IT15.8.4 — OPENING DISPERSION / HOUSE ORDER
========================================================

BASELINE
- Directly built on IT15.8.3. No rewrite or forward-port from the failed 16.x line.

REPLAY FINDINGS
- Moving the no-idle watchdog before normal assignment in IT15.8.3 stopped same-turn
  command replacement, but made the watchdog assign every initially idle food civilian
  to the same nearest berry bush before the least-loaded allocator ran.
- House #2 was committed immediately after House #1 completed at roughly 30/40 pop,
  while Barracks #1 remained unplaced.

CORRECTIONS
1. Opening berry dispersion without command thrashing
   - Normal assignment again runs first.
   - A per-update ownership set records every worker the normal allocator handled.
   - The watchdog runs second and skips those workers, while retaining authority over
     genuinely unhandled idle civilians.
2. Barracks before House #2
   - Before a live Barracks foundation, second-House actions and unstarted queue plans
     are held whenever more than three population spaces remain.
   - Existing House foundations are never cancelled; <=3 free population is an adaptive
     emergency escape.
3. House locality and protected space
   - The first House prefers the CC-side of a wood Storehouse, away from the woodline.
   - Later Houses consider the outside of established farm districts before extending
     the old compact House block.
   - Existing resource-corridor, Storehouse-ring, and reserved Field-slot vetoes remain.

DEFERRED
- Building garrison/ungarrison path shortening is feasible, but is intentionally not
  mixed into this opening correction. It needs its own path-benefit, capacity, threat,
  and successful-unload state machine.
