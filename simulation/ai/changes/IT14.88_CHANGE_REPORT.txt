IT14.88 CHANGE REPORT

Surgical combat/logistics follow-up from IT14.87. The successful IT14.86 Farmstead scoring and IT14.87 wood-crisis/P3-FINISH repairs remain intact.
Only three Petra JavaScript files change: attackManager.js, expertDecisionController.js and expertDecision/policy.js.

1. EXPERT SIEGE — NO AUTOMATIC BOLT SHOOTERS
- Expert automatic finishing/P3 siege selection now rejects Oxybeles/bolt-shooter/ballista templates.
- Rams remain the first-choice building breaker. If a civ has a suitable non-ram mechanical building siege unit, the second P3 engine can be that catapult; otherwise another ram is valid.
- Unit templates and human-player availability are unchanged.
- General attack target selection, movement, retreat and FINISH logic are NOT rewritten in this version.

2. SMALL HEALER ESCORT
- Expert can maintain a small support detachment once its military is large enough: target 2 healers in Town, 3 in City.
- Healers use a dedicated high-priority support queue with healthy resource reserves; they do not replace the required combat army.
- Healers deliberately remain outside AttackPlan.unitCollection so they cannot satisfy launch-size/local-balance checks and are not given attack orders.
- The AttackManager places them on a defensive trailing line behind the largest live offensive, preserves active healing inside the leash, and periodically re-escorts stragglers.
- Existing Athens Hippocrates behavior remains valid and a healer hero counts toward the small support total.

3. ATHENS — IPHICRATEAN REFORMS IS OPTIONAL, NOT A MAX-TECH GATE
- Iphicratean Reforms is explicitly excluded from the generic military-tech lanes and the P3 max-tech completion gate.
- A separate Athens-only City rule may research it when mandatory military tech is complete, the army is meaningfully melee-heavy (>=20 melee infantry and >=45% melee share), the opponent is not already in cleanup range, wood is stable, and the live bank can pay the tech while retaining large operating reserves.
- The dedicated optional queue can research during/around the final package but never delays the P3 all-in.
- The rule reads the live technology cost rather than hard-coding it.

4. WOOD STOREHOUSE — ACTUAL LUMBERJACK DISTRICT SAFETY NET
- The generic resource-service audit now includes wood, independent of primaryWoodWorksite.
- If >=3 actual lumberjacks are working an owned local district with >=450 nearby wood and their dropsite is >12m away or measured round trip exceeds ~4s, Expert can request a Storehouse centered on those workers' actual tree targets.
- Nearby local trees are summed around the worker district before judging payback, so a rich forest is not mistaken for a tiny fragment merely because current workers target only a few individual trees.
- This directly addresses the observed case where workers left a serviced green wood area for an unserved red woodline while the old primary-worksite state lagged behind.

5. FARMSTEAD / STOREHOUSE COEXISTENCE
- The opening Storehouse remains fully protected by the existing opening geometry.
- Later wood/service Storehouses may use the EDGE of a farm district instead of being rejected solely by the old blanket 28m Farmstead radius.
- Before the normal 10-field economy is complete, every proven/potential Field footprint is still reserved: a Storehouse may not consume a farm slot or overlap the Farmstead.
- After 10 real Fields exist, later Storehouses stop reserving hypothetical future Field slots; real Fields/Farmsteads remain protected by the engine obstruction map.
- Farmstead placement, field packing, field materialization and food-capacity logic themselves are unchanged.

6. FROZEN / NON-REGRESSION
- IT14.86 opening Farmstead multi-bush scoring unchanged.
- IT14.87 practical wood-district continuity, fragment rejection, wood-crisis expansion and Athens Cleruchy scarcity logic unchanged.
- IT14.87 P3 FINISH/pop-delta persistence unchanged.
- Field packing/materialization/watchdog unchanged.
- Farmstead #2/#3 capacity/deadlock logic unchanged.
- House snapping/eco protection and CC civilian rules unchanged.
- Opening Storehouse selection unchanged.
- General attack movement/target-selection logic unchanged.
