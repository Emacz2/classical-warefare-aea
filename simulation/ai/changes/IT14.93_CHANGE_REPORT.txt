EXPERT AI PETRA — IT14.93 CHANGE REPORT
=======================================

PURPOSE
IT14.93 is a surgical non-regression repair of the IT14.92 Eurasian Steppe test from
2026-09-11. Two failures are addressed only:
1) opening Storehouse selection chose a small near-CC wood clump, then immediately paid
   for a better second Storehouse;
2) the IT14.92 live cutting-front records could reach summarizeWoodTrees without a finite
   dropDistance, producing repeated woodsite.averageDropDistance finite-number errors.

1) OPENING STOREHOUSE: BEST SUSTAINABLE FOREST FIRST
Previous behavior:
- opening wood search was only 90m from the CC;
- the one-time CC approach walk still carried enough score penalty that a small close
  clump could be preferred;
- on the reference replay Storehouse #1 finished, then Expert immediately admitted the
  district was weak and queued Storehouse #2.

IT14.93:
- opening wood search radius: 180m on the same land access and owned territory;
- opening cluster scoring radius: 40m;
- CC approach penalty reduced sharply (0.35 weight);
- total usable wood / tree capacity dominates the choice;
- repeated worker drop distance still matters;
- opening diagnostic:
    [EXPERT-WOOD-OPEN] selected wood=... approach=... avgDrop=... candidates=...

Intent:
A 1500-3000 wood forest a little farther from the CC should beat a 300-500 wood clump
near the CC. The builders make the longer walk once; lumberjacks use the Storehouse for
hundreds of later drops.

2) SECOND STOREHOUSE HANDOFF GRACE
Previous behavior:
Immediately after Storehouse #1 completed, its builders had not yet become active
lumberjacks. The wood planner saw active=0 and could interpret that transient handoff as
an emergency, releasing the district and queuing Storehouse #2.

IT14.93:
- record the completion time of Storehouses;
- after the opening Storehouse completes, no second Storehouse placement is authorized
  for 45 seconds;
- this allows the selected opening forest and its worker crew to establish before any
  second dropsite can be economically judged;
- state is serialized/deserialized for save-game consistency.

This is deliberately a grace period, not a permanent one-Storehouse rule. Later
Storehouses remain legal when real measured economics justify them.

3) FIX woodsite.averageDropDistance ERROR SPAM
Root cause:
- collectInitialWoodCandidates() records contain position/remaining/saturation but no
  dropDistance;
- IT14.92's live cutting-front path reused those records in summarizeWoodTrees();
- undefined dropDistance propagated to NaN averageDropDistance;
- petraApiAdapter correctly rejected the non-finite observation and the Expert update
  aborted repeatedly.

IT14.93:
- every live-front tree is normalized with a finite dropDistance before it can enter the
  ordinary woodsite summarizer;
- nearest live wood dropsite distance is used when available;
- current primary worksite distance is a finite fallback;
- computed averageDropDistance is always finite (0 only when no weighted wood exists).

IT14.92 Steppe replay produced hundreds of:

IT14.93 must produce zero instances of that error.
