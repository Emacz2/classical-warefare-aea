CWA Petra Expert IT16.2
========================

Baseline
--------
IT16.2 was rebuilt from the known-working IT16.0 source. The broken IT16.1/16.1b
branches were not used as a release base.

Corrections
-----------
1. Opening fruit distribution now counts live gather-order targets as well as
   supply metadata. Berry/date-palm patches fill one civilian per source before
   stacking.
2. Field #1 is blocked until Barracks #1 is built, founded, or queued. The
   existing natural-food hold still applies independently.
3. Low-wood Athens P1 adaptation is reevaluated during play. It can reserve the
   Forge, unlock Slingers without an extra stone cushion, and avoid the ordinary
   melee-tech package blocking that unlock.
4. The recovery Market uses its full 200W/25S/25M affordability test. A Market
   is also a bounded two-Field food hub and placement scoring preserves those
   two faces.
5. Farmstead hubs remain four-Field hubs, discouraging redundant half-used
   Farmsteads. Temple and other reserved placement uses the combined Farmstead
   and Market farm-slot geometry.
6. A marching Expert army of 20+ units pulls members more than 80 metres from
   the formation centre back before allowing another advance step.
7. Existing IT16.0 rules remain intact, including the 60-civilian CC gate,
   immediate production-lane refill, and two-Forge normal cap.

Release note
------------
This package has passed whole-tree JavaScript parsing, an executable
field-order/natural-food smoke test, line-ending validation, archive integrity,
and post-extraction module parsing. Replay behavior still requires in-game
testing because the full 0 A.D. simulation engine is not available here.
