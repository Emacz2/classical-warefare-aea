IT14.84 OPENING FARMSTEAD ROBUSTNESS PASS

Baseline: IT14.83. Military escalation/siege changes from 14.83 are preserved unchanged.

Problem confirmed in IT14.83 vs Seleucids
- Opening Farmstead was accepted with hubCap=0+0 but geometric g4.
- The geometric g4 check ignores live obstructions by design, so permanent rocks/metal/trees can look like future farm space.
- After natural food cleared, that Farmstead could support only 1 Field.
- EconomyPlanner then required 2 Fields from the bad opening hub before allowing Farmstead #2, creating a circular permanent-food deadlock.

14.84 changes
- Opening Farmstead must have at least 1 genuinely legal Field slot at placement time; 2 live slots are strongly preferred.
- The old future/geometric four-slot estimate remains only a secondary signal for berry ground that will later clear.
- After repeated genuine placement failures, the opening may still fall back so unusual maps cannot hard-lock minute 0.
- Once natural food is gone, if the single opening hub has zero open Field slots and fields are still missing, Farmstead #2 is authorized immediately even if hub #1 produced only 0-1 Fields.
- Permanent follow-up hubs retain the existing 3-4 Field compact-placement requirement.

Frozen / unchanged
- 14.83 P2 attack escalation and siege-replacement logic.
- Field placement geometry/materialization watchdog from 14.82.
- 40% natural-food transition, max 3 Farmsteads, CC training rules, 25/25/25/25 doctrines, barter, FINISH and defense fixes.
