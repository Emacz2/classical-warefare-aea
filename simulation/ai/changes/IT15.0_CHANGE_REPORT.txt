EXPERT AI PETRA — IT15.0 CHANGE REPORT
=====================================

PURPOSE
-------
IT15.0 is the efficiency/ownership pass built directly from the two IT14.99 replays.
It treats "NO IDLE" as a fundamental invariant for both economic units and production
buildings, and makes rush civilian labor follow the Steven-derived model: dedicated
civilians own food while citizen-soldiers carry secondary resources until mobilization.

1. RUSH CIVILIANS BECOME THE FOOD BACKBONE
-------------------------------------------
- Early P1 Rush soft civilian target: 34 (recovery ceiling 40).
- Late P1 Rush soft civilian target: 36 (recovery ceiling 42).
- These rush caps persist through Town Phase instead of expiring on a timer or when the
  first attack launches. City Phase / explicit rush recovery can reopen the global 65 cap.
- Rush doctrines train only the opening three dedicated civilian lumberjacks.
- New dedicated civilians after the opening become food-owned/farm labor.
- Once six permanent Fields exist, the three opening civilian lumberjacks are explicitly
  migrated onto food. Their old permanent-resource lease cannot veto this handoff.
- Rush civilian surplus logic no longer grows a civilian wood workforce; citizen-soldiers
  are expected to carry wood/stone/metal.

2. FARMSTEAD BUILDERS OWN THEIR LOCAL FOOD DISTRICT
---------------------------------------------------
- A civilian committed to a natural-food Farmstead may not be sent across the map by a
  generic fallback while its local district still has useful work.
- Farmstead district ownership outranks stale temporary wood leases.
- The no-idle rescue path respects the same locality contract instead of overriding it.
- Safe neutral fruit/berries immediately beside a Farmstead are valid district food. This
  fixes the contradiction where Petra built a Farmstead to service neutral food and then
  ignored that food because the gatherer filter required owned territory.

3. PERMANENT FOOD CAPACITY / FIELD DEADLOCK FIX
-----------------------------------------------
- Zero legal field slots is now authoritative evidence that the current Farmstead network
  is capacity-blocked.
- With one or two Farmsteads, if desired Fields are missing and natural food has fallen to
  25% or less, Petra may build a dedicated farming hub immediately.
- The old circular requirement (two Farmsteads needed two existing Fields before a third
  farming hub could be allowed) is removed.
- Rush field floors are raised to six Fields by ~45 population and eight by ~75 population,
  subject to resources/placement legality.

4. WICKER BASKETS RECOGNIZES THE REAL FRUIT NETWORK
---------------------------------------------------
- Wicker priority no longer depends only on connected owned-territory cluster count.
- The opening scan counts safe same-land owned OR neutral fruit within the local opening
  region, so two nearby patches cannot merge/disappear and accidentally let Iron Axe win.

5. NO-IDLE MILITARY PRODUCTION / UNIT SUBSTITUTION
--------------------------------------------------
- Protecting wood now reserves up to 350 wood rather than pretending the entire wood bank
  is unavailable.
- If the protected-budget preference cannot train a unit but the live bank can afford any
  valid citizen-soldier, Petra immediately reselects against the live bank and trains an
  affordable substitute.
- Unfunded standby plans are re-evaluated. A standby Swordsman waiting for metal is removed
  if an affordable Hoplite/Javelineer/etc. can be trained now.
- Preferences are therefore composition goals, never permission for an idle Barracks.

6. FOOD-CRISIS REBALANCING IS FASTER AND USES ACTUAL WORK
---------------------------------------------------------
- Food-recovery civilian batch: up to 6 workers every 6 seconds (was 2 every 12 seconds).
- Catastrophic food (<150) with excess wood (>=1000) may trigger recovery even before the
  normal capacity feedback catches up.
- Rebalancing identifies workers actually chopping wood from their live supply/order, not
  only stale permanent job metadata.
- Emergency reserve-soldier food support target rises from 6 to 8.

7. EXPERT OWNS HOUSE UNGARRISON
-------------------------------
- Civilians garrisoned by Expert safety logic are explicitly unloaded from Houses/CCs when
  the local threat has been clear for 10 seconds.
- Expert no longer assumes the generic Petra garrison manager will eventually release them.

8. STOREHOUSE DISCIPLINE
------------------------
- Healthy wood bank + rich serviced forest blocks speculative new Storehouses.
- Dense-forest deepening requires the bank to be low enough to justify it.
- A same-patch second Storehouse must itself service a meaningful local wood pocket
  (minimum ~1200), preventing the IT14.99 35k-current-wood -> 600-wood candidate expansion.
- Existing emergency/continuity escape logic remains intact so IT14.86's "never open a new
  woodline" regression is not reintroduced.

PRESERVED FROM IT14.99
----------------------
- Farmstead future-ring placement scoring.
- Early-P1 timing-window launch fallback.
- Hard casualty/attrition budget.
- Athens rich-economy champion core (P2 12 champion hoplites + 5 champion javelineers;
  P3 15 + 5) and no deliberate champion-crossbow replenishment.
- IT14.98 Late-P1 64 preferred / 52 minimum timing-army framework.
- Canonical benchmark methodology and decisive-defeat timing rule.

WHAT TO WATCH IN TESTING
------------------------
1. By the 6-8 minute range on a rush build, dedicated civilians should be overwhelmingly on
   food/farms, while citizen-soldiers perform most wood/mineral gathering.
2. After six Fields complete, look for [EXPERT-FOOD-BACKBONE] moving the opening civilian
   lumber trio to food.
3. Farmstead builders should remain in their local food district; look for [EXPERT-LOCALITY].
4. Barracks should not wait for metal-heavy Swordsmen while an affordable Hoplite/Javelineer
   exists; look for mode=no-idle-substitute or [EXPERT-TRAIN] replace-standby.
5. Civilians sheltered in Houses should log [EXPERT-CIV] ungarrison after the threat clears.
6. A rich serviced forest should log healthy/hold behavior instead of buying a tiny second
   Storehouse.
