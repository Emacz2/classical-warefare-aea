CWA Petra Expert AI — IT15.3 Change Report
==========================================

Purpose
-------
IT15.3 is the worker-efficiency / research-throughput correction built from the
2026-09-12 Human-vs-Expert comparison. It preserves IT15.2's repaired permanent-
food opening and territorial sovereignty while attacking the measured reason a
human without the Expert gather bonus could still out-gather Expert: too much
worker travel/reassignment and too little parallel research throughput.

1. RESTORED FORECAST STOREHOUSE HELPERS (runtime error fix)
-----------------------------------------------------------
IT15.1 removed resourceWorkersBySupply() and forecastStorehouseCandidate() while
applyForecastStorehouseInfrastructure() continued to call the latter. IT15.2
therefore threw:

  this.forecastStorehouseCandidate is not a function

IT15.3 restores both helpers. The restored candidate scan is updated for the
IT15.1 sovereignty contract: only PlayerID-owned resources may become immediate
Storehouse-service candidates. Neutral resources remain expansion intelligence.

2. PRODUCTIVE WORKER INERTIA / GLOBAL REASSIGNMENT CHURN
---------------------------------------------------------
The Human-vs-Expert replay showed Expert repeatedly peeling large groups between
resources. At one sample 55 workers were walking; a +35% gather bonus cannot pay
while workers are crossing the map.

IT15.3 changes forecast rebalancing:
- workers already APPROACHING/WALKING/RETURNING are not turned around by a new
  forecast signal unless correcting a temporary metadata/resource mismatch;
- idle/mismatched/nonproductive workers move before productive gatherers;
- productive gatherers are capped at 2 strategic moves per 12-second window;
- even forecast-critical rebalancing is capped at 5 total workers per pass;
- workers recently strategically reassigned have a 12-second anti-churn lease.

This does not freeze resource balance. It changes the order of operations to:
new/idle/flexible labor first, productive specialists last.

3. WOODLINE CONTINUITY
----------------------
- woodMigrationSalvageRadius increased from 52m to 68m so the same forest can be
  followed farther before Petra declares that district finished;
- staged migration batch reduced from 4 to 2 workers per 12-second window;
- existing explicit WORKSITE_ID behavior remains: a new Storehouse is primarily
  for new workers; productive workers stay on their old viable district.

The 14.86 non-regression rule remains: continuity is a preference, never a veto.
If the old district is genuinely exhausted/unusable, Expert may still open/use a
new owned-territory district.

4. BARRACKS BATCH TRAINING
--------------------------
Barracks now request efficient batches of 2, or 3 when the food bank is healthy.
queueExpertSoldierBatch() still shrinks the request to the largest immediately
affordable batch, including 1, so batching can never create trainer idle time.

5. P1 RUSH REINFORCEMENT ECONOMY
--------------------------------
After a Rush plan has launched, newly trained citizen-soldiers are no longer
pre-owned by that started rush. They remain available to the home economy rather
than walking across the map 1-3 at a time. Existing wounded-replacement logic may
still send a deliberate replacement wave when the field army actually needs it.
This supports the intended doctrine: pressure once; if the exchange turns bad,
retreat/reboom with a stronger home economy instead of feeding the fight.

6. P3 BOOM: PARALLEL FORGES + EARLIER MILITARY RESEARCH
--------------------------------------------------------
The max-tech requirement is NOT relaxed. P3 Boom still wants all relevant
military technology completed before its normal all-in.

IT15.3 makes that achievable earlier:
- P3 begins Forge military research in Town phase while economic research
  continues in its own research buildings;
- P3 Forge #1 may come online from ~70 population / ~5:30 timing when normal
  infrastructure conditions are met;
- Forge #2 is proactively justified by a real multi-tech backlog, not only after
  Forge #1 has already become a visible bottleneck;
- P3 may build Forge #3 around 105 population / 8 Fields when the military tech
  backlog and live bank justify a third research lane;
- military research queues now support three Forge lanes;
- P3 max-tech mode no longer silently skips narrow relevant military technologies
  merely because the current army has few cavalry/javelineers.

The objective is not "attack with missing techs at 14:00." It is "use the boom
and 2-3 Forges so the tech tree is already finished when the ~14:00 army is ready."

7. IPHICRATES SUPPORT BEHAVIOR
-------------------------------
Iphicrates now has Expert-only command-hero support behavior:
- attaches to the largest active primary army instead of remaining at home;
- defensive stance and centre/back-centre leash to keep his aura with the army;
- withdraws around 62% health and does not rejoin until ~85% health;
- survival is prioritized over extra hero DPS.

8. PRESERVED IT15.2 CONTRACTS
------------------------------
Unchanged in intent:
- no intentional AWAIT_FOOD_INFRASTRUCTURE idle state;
- idle/overflow food civilians force permanent food capacity and remain productive;
- dedicated rush civilians are food-focused;
- economic gathering targets must be inside PlayerID territory;
- neutral resources are not immediately accessible economic stock;
- food-capacity debt suppresses speculative mineral Storehouses but not required
  owned-wood rollover;
- attackPlan.js is unchanged;
- EXPERT_BENCHMARKS.txt is unchanged.
