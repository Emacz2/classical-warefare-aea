IT14.86 CHANGE REPORT

Focused efficiency pass from the fixed IT14.85 baseline.
Only expertDecisionController.js and expertDecision/policy.js change among Petra JavaScript files.

1. OPENING FARMSTEAD — MULTI-BUSH SERVICE
- The old opening score heavily rewarded the single nearest berry bush.
- That could choose a Farmstead touching one bush while two neighboring bushes were several metres farther away.
- IT14.86 ranks otherwise-legal opening positions by the three closest natural-food sources, rewarding positions that service 2-3 bushes together.
- Existing hard safeguards are unchanged: live Field-slot minimum/preference, future Field geometry, territory/access legality and the 135-degree farm orientation remain intact.

2. WOODLINE ROLLOVER — CURRENT PATCH FIRST
- When the wood planner requests another dropsite, Expert now gives a whittled current forest one late same-patch improvement opportunity even after it falls below the old 1200-wood deepen threshold.
- This late reuse is limited to the first additional Storehouse on that connected forest and requires a real drop-distance improvement.
- If the current serviced forest still has at least ~450 wood, Expert waits rather than opening a fresh remote woodline merely because no same-patch improvement is worthwhile yet.
- Once the current forest is genuinely low, the normal new-patch search resumes.
- A measured phase/wood-income continuity emergency bypasses the hold, so this cannot intentionally starve a phase-up or a stalled lumber economy.

3. NON-REGRESSION
- Field packing/materialization/watchdog code unchanged.
- Farmstead #2/#3 capacity/deadlock rules unchanged.
- House snap/eco-protection code unchanged.
- P2/P3 attack, siege, FINISH and research logic unchanged.
- CC civilian rules and 25/25/25/25 doctrine weights unchanged.
- IT14.85 assignFarmWorker position hotfix preserved unchanged.
