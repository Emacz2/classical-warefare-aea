PETRA EXPERT IT15.8.10 — AUTHORITATIVE OPENING WOODSITE

Replay diagnosis
- The preferred 1,600-wood site was selected correctly, but all 258 opening Storehouse candidates were rejected.
- Opening Storehouses preserved a blanket Farmstead-distance radius even when they did not overlap a real reserved Field footprint.
- Recovery searched alternate forests while placement scoring and worker targeting remained bound to the original forest.
- New civilians therefore walked toward the stale tree target, then were redirected after the recovered Storehouse completed.
- Teleport garrison commands were issued, but construction repair commands replaced them before the engine completed garrisoning.

Corrections
- Opening Storehouses may use the Farmstead district edge; every exact current/future Field rectangle remains protected.
- Every recovery candidate carries its own woodsite/tree/resource identity through placement scoring.
- The chosen candidate index is returned by the mechanical coordinator and becomes the single authoritative opening woodsite immediately.
- The recovered Storehouse foundation becomes the destination for new wood workers before completion, preventing stale cross-forest assignments.
- Placement failures now report rejection-type counts rather than only a total.
- A pending CC pass-through owns its builder until physical unload completes or a four-second garrison timeout aborts safely.
- Construction repair orders cannot overwrite a pending pass-through command.

Preserved
- IT15.8.9 construction/no-idle ownership, civilian-only food work, and compact House priority.
- IT15.8.8 staged-forest valuation.
- IT15.8.7 Farmstead-builder food ownership.
