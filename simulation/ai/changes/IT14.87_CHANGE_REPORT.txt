IT14.87 CHANGE REPORT

Surgical repair from IT14.86. The IT14.86 opening Farmstead improvement is frozen unchanged.
Only three Petra JavaScript files change: attackManager.js, expertDecisionController.js and expertDecision/policy.js.

1. WOOD ACCESS / EXPANSION — CONTINUITY IS A PREFERENCE, NOT A VETO
- Keeps the IT14.86 idea of working an existing serviced wood district efficiently, but removes the hard "connected forest >= 450" veto that could strand workers.
- A practical wood district now includes one nearby fringe hop beyond strict graph-connected trees. Small clumps separated by a narrow gap can be treated as the same economic district.
- If no useful same-district Storehouse pad exists, Expert is allowed to search the next in-territory forest immediately instead of returning no placement.
- A fast crisis override now fires when wood bank is <=250 and active woodcutters are <=2; the existing measured-delivery/zero-active watchdog and phase-wood crisis also remain authoritative.
- Crisis search radius is widened for a real forest, while tiny new districts are rejected (normal minimum ~600 wood; emergency minimum ~350) rather than spending a Storehouse on 100-200 wood fragments.
- Athens scarcity expansion now treats a funded wood crisis as a Cleruchy signal. A rich wood-only neutral frontier can qualify during scarcity, so a large forest does not need a second resource type beside it to be considered.

2. P3 MAX-TECH ALL-IN — NET ENEMY POPULATION NO LONGER CAUSES FALSE RETREAT
- Ordinary Rush/P2/P3 bad-exchange protection remains unchanged outside the P3 Boom Max-Tech All-In doctrine.
- During P3 Max-Tech All-In, enemy population delta is still observed for diagnostics but cannot abort a favorable local battle by itself.
- The replay cases 88v70 with 12 losses / enemyDamage=2 and 82v58 with 12 losses / enemyDamage=1 now remain committed.
- A bad-exchange abort during this doctrine requires both substantial own losses (>=30% of launch size) AND an actual collapse: local enemy superiority or the field army falling to roughly <=45% of launch size.
- Existing depleted-army, broken-screen, catastrophic-outnumber, siege, capture and FINISH systems are otherwise left intact.

3. FROZEN / NON-REGRESSION
- IT14.86 opening Farmstead multi-bush scoring unchanged.
- Field packing/materialization/watchdog unchanged.
- Farmstead #2/#3 geometry/capacity/deadlock rules unchanged.
- House snapping and eco-protection unchanged.
- CC civilian rules unchanged.
- Opening Storehouse selection unchanged apart from the later wood-district rollover logic above.
- Doctrine weights, research sequencing and general siege production unchanged.
