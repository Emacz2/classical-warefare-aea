EXPERT AI PETRA — IT14.98 CHANGE REPORT
=======================================

PURPOSE
IT14.98 converts the Late-P1 doctrine from a civilian-heavy boom that merely *labels* itself a rush into a real economic-military timing. The opening Farmstead/Storehouse work from IT14.97 is intentionally untouched. This build focuses on population composition, food/wood throughput, CC military conversion, launch authority and P2 timing.

1) GLOBAL CIVILIAN CEILING = 65
- Global permanent-civilian ceiling reduced from 70 to 65.
- P2/P3 doctrines use 65 as their normal CC civilian target.
- This is NOT implemented as a blind Late-P1 cut: Late-P1 has its own lower, economy-conditioned conversion target.

2) LATE-P1 CIVILIAN TARGET = 48, RECOVERY BAND TO 55
- Late-P1 normally stops dedicated civilians at 48 so the CC can join Barracks production.
- If food/wood is genuinely short AND existing workers are productive (>=90% not idle/unproductive, <=25% approaching) AND there is actual labor capacity that another civilian can use, the CC may temporarily continue to 55.
- Food shortage with zero open food capacity does NOT trigger more civilians.
- Stalled/crisis wood access does NOT trigger more civilians. Those remain infrastructure/assignment problems.
- Once the timing army reaches 75% of its fixed launch package, recovery civilians stop stealing CC cycles.
- After the rush launches or is abandoned, the economy may rebuild toward the global 65 ceiling.

3) CC SOLDIER FOOD BURN IS NOW INCLUDED IN ECONOMIC PLANNING
Previous behavior:
- when the CC reached its civilian target, modeled CC food burn fell to zero;
- if the CC then trained soldiers, the food planner still budgeted only the Barracks lanes.

IT14.98:
- after civilian conversion, the food-throughput model estimates one continuous CC citizen-soldier lane;
- two Barracks + CC therefore plan food capacity for all three active production buildings.

This is the core catch-22 fix: we can lower dedicated civilians only if the economy knows the real production bill it must sustain.

4) LATE-P1 ECONOMY IS FOOD/WOOD FIRST
- While Late-P1 is live and unlaunched in Village Phase, normal worker balancing targets food + wood.
- Stone/metal become legal targets only when an already-queued plan has an immediate cost for that resource.
- This prevents a timing build from parking large worker groups on speculative P2 stone/metal while food production is starving.
- Existing forecast/rebalance logic remains responsible for moving surplus miners back toward the live shortage.

5) REAL LATE-P1 ARMY: 64 PREFERRED / 52 MINIMUM
- Late-P1 preferred army increased from 28 to 64.
- Fixed minimum launch package = 52.
- Athens 58% melee share produces an intended 37 melee / 27 ranged package, with roughly 30 melee / 22 ranged at the 52-man minimum.
- Barracks still begin at the existing timing; the CC becomes the extra production lane once the live civilian target is reached.

6) RUSH GATE NO LONGER CHASES A MOVING GLOBAL TARGET
Previous behavior:
- known enemy combat population continually raised the required package;
- in the reference replay the gate moved from needed=9 through the 30s/40s/50s while the AI army tried to catch it;
- enemy total population could also veto the timing after the army had already matured.

IT14.98:
- Late-P1 must first reach the fixed 52-man package;
- known enemy army requirement is capped at that fixed package instead of growing forever;
- total enemy population no longer vetoes a Late-P1 once 52 attackers exist;
- LOCAL defenders/static defenses remain authoritative, so the AI still refuses an obviously suicidal defended-base dive;
- Athens' P1 melee upgrade remains valuable, but a complete 52-man timing package is no longer held forever solely because that upgrade is missing.

7) LATE-P1 OWNS THE P1 -> P2 TIMING WINDOW
Previous behavior:
- generic `absolute-7m` phase logic could queue Town while Late-P1 was still arming;
- the phase reservation then helped cancel the selected rush around the exact timing window it was meant to exploit.

IT14.98:
- while an unlaunched Late-P1 remains live, ALL generic P2 readiness lanes are held;
- the hold ends when the rush launches, is explicitly abandoned/recovery mode starts, or the 570s (9:30) opportunity deadline expires;
- after launch/abandonment, normal P2 follow-up resumes.

8) FROZEN / NON-REGRESSION
Unchanged from IT14.97:

- opening Storehouse best-serviceable-forest selection;
- neutral border wood servicing;
- later wood district continuity/rollover logic;
- house placement;
- field packing/materialization/watchdogs;
- attackPlan.js;
