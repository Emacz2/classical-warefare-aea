CWA Petra Expert AI — IT15.9 Change Report

Base: IT15.8. IT15.9 is a conservative consistency patch for the +30% Expert calibration.
The record-setting opening, attack authorization/execution, civilian calibration and
economic-safety kernel remain frozen.

REPLAY EVIDENCE
- The same IT15.8 build ranged from a sub-15-minute victory to 20:30 and 24:55.4 wins.
- The slow second replay began Fields correctly at 24% natural food, but the sampled
  natural ratio later rebounded to 25-26%. The natural-first hold re-engaged and froze
  the mature population staircase at six Fields.
- The same replay left reachable grapes while permanent farming began. This overlap is
  legal and desirable; abandoning the grapes is not. Natural gatherers remain eligible
  while the permanent Field pipeline continues.
- Multiple runs ended with three Forges despite little/no useful parallel research.

CHANGES
1. One-way natural-to-permanent-food transition.
   - Natural-first remains a hard hold before the first Field enters the pipeline.
   - Once any Field is built, founded or queued, the transition is latched.
   - A noisy 24% -> 26% natural-food reading cannot freeze later 8/10-Field targets.
   - Remaining safe serviced grapes/berries remain valid and are gathered alongside Fields.

2. Mature Field progress restored.
   - The existing population staircase remains 6 Fields at 70 pop, 8 at 90 and 10 at 120.
   - The Field progress watchdog uses the same latched transition rule.
   - Ten remains the normal mature target; Fields 11-12 remain emergency reserve capacity.

3. Global one-Forge limit.
   - P3 boom no longer prebuilds Forge #2/#3 from a technology-count/resource-bank guess.
   - All relevant military research is serialized through the first global Forge.
   - Secondary bases/colonies cannot manufacture redundant parallel Forge capacity.

FROZEN / PRESERVED
- +30% Expert gather/trade bonus.
- 60-civilian one-time growth ceiling and mature food-backbone replacement rule.
- IT15.6 record P2 combat, launch/opportunity and finishing behavior.
- Opening Farmstead/Storehouse, Wicker branch, cavalry/chickens and housing.
- IT15.8 wood-safety kernel, neutral rescue, barter and low-wood unit substitution.
- Barracks #2 food-capacity gate and P2/P3 production timing.

TEST EXPECTATIONS
- No more than one Forge.
- A game that reaches 120 population after starting the farm transition continues toward
  ten Fields even when reachable natural food remains or its measured ratio rebounds.
- Existing safe natural-food workers are not deliberately stripped solely because Fields exist.
- Favorable-seed sub-15 potential is preserved; slow seeds should no longer stop at six Fields.
