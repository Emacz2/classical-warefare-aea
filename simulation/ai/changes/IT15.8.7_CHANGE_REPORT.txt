Baseline

Corrections
- Distant natural-food clusters that economically warrant a Farmstead cannot receive ordinary food workers until a nearby Farmstead foundation exists.

- The Wicker branch Farmstead timer begins only after Barracks #1 is placed, preventing the opening gate from causing a timeout and premature long-haul release.

- The opening Farmstead task retains its selected natural-food cluster through completion; its four civilian builders are then locked to and dispersed across those supplies.
- Dropsite service is measured against the actual food supplies rather than only the cluster centroid, so a correctly placed edge Farmstead is recognized.
