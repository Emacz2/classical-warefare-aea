EXPERT AI PETRA — IT14.95 CHANGE REPORT
=======================================

PURPOSE
IT14.95 is a narrow production/food-capacity correction based on the 2026-09-11
Athens Expert replay. IT14.93 opening/resource work and IT14.94 pressure/defense/
repeat-expansion changes are intentionally preserved.

The replay exposed three specific issues:
1) Field #6 was delayed for minutes despite food:critical, no natural food and a
   watchdog that had already requested the next Field;
2) Early-P1 doctrine selection allowed the Civic Centre to interleave soldiers at
   only ~42 civilians, slowing the economic target and starving the future-farmer
   pipeline;
3) a completed Cleruchy successfully expanded territory but then sat idle instead
   of contributing military production.

1) FIELD PROGRESS WATCHDOG: REQUEST != PROGRESS
Previous behavior:
- the watchdog correctly detected a stalled Field pipeline;
- merely INSERTING a rescue Field action reset lastFieldPipelineProgressAt;
- if the future-farmer crew layer then rejected/delayed that action, the watchdog
  believed it had made progress and waited another interval;
- in the reference replay Expert sat at 5 Fields from roughly 6:07 to ~9:30 while
  food was critical and natural food was gone.

IT14.95:
- lastFieldPipelineProgressAt is reset ONLY when the real pipeline increases
  (built Field + Field foundation + queued Field);
- a rescue request that fails to acquire a valid crew does not hide the stall;
- the existing 3-4 newly-trained future-farmer crew rule remains unchanged;
- the 2-builder food-emergency escape remains an emergency only.

Expected diagnostic:
  [EXPERT-FIELD-WATCH] force next field ...
followed promptly by:
  [EXPERT-FARM-CREW] field ... futureBuilders=... stay=farmer
and a real queued/foundation Field.

2) GLOBAL CIVIC-CENTRE CIVILIAN TARGET = 70
Previous behavior:
- P2/P3 already protected CC civilian production to 70;
- P1 rush doctrines could lower the practical CC target and begin cc-rush military
  pulses while only ~42 civilians existed;
- that slowed civilian growth and delayed the new-civilian farm pipeline.

IT14.95 contract:
- EVERY doctrine has a CC civilian target of 70;
- below 70 the CC trains civilians by default;
- selecting or merely arming a P1 doctrine is NOT an exception;
- one-unit CC military pulses below 70 are allowed only for a real combat state:
    * meaningful base defense;
    * an attack already on the field;
    * a P1 rush already near its real launch package (~75% / minimum 12);
    * an existing P2/P3 major attack that is actually near launch;
- at 70 civilians the CC may join normal military production;
- optional pursuit/hunting cavalry from the CC is also blocked until 70 civilians.

Barracks and Cleruchies remain free to train soldiers before 70.

Expected diagnostics:
  [EXPERT-PRODUCTION] cc-continuity ... cap=70 ...
No ordinary [EXPERT-MIL] queued cc-* soldiers below 70 unless accompanied by:
  [EXPERT-CC] military-exception reason=... civilians=X/70

3) COMPLETED CLERUCHIES TRAIN MILITARY UNITS
Previous behavior:
- IT14.94 could finally build/rebuild a useful Cleruchy;
- after completion the Cleruchy was not part of Expert's continuous military
  production loop and could remain idle.

IT14.95:
- every completed Athens Cleruchy is enrolled as a forward military trainer;
- it dynamically reads the current CWA trainable roster through the same existing
  CitizenSoldier selector used by normal Expert infantry production;
- queue depth is shallow (2), one unit per refill, so it supplements rather than
  overwhelms Barracks composition/resource planning;
- it inherits normal population, resource, combat-ownership and police gating.

Expected diagnostics after Cleruchy completion:
  [EXPERT-MIL] queued cleruchy-auto soldiers=... trainer=...
  [EXPERT-TRAIN] cleruchy-auto trainer=... depth=.../2
