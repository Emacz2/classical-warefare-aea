PETRA EXPERT IT15.8.1 — CORRECTIVE CHANGE REPORT
================================================

BASELINE
- Built directly from the untouched IT15.8 tree.
- No rewrite and no merge from IT15.9/16.x.
- Expert gather/trade bonus remains +30%; civilian growth ceiling remains 60.

CORRECTIONS
1. Existing farm capacity before another Farmstead
   - The final capacity pass now applies before any additional permanent farm hub.
   - It clears transient failed-slot memory for existing hubs and reruns the full compact scanner.

2. Conditional Wicker Baskets
   - The ordinary starting fruit patch no longer qualifies by itself.
   - Wicker requires multiple worthwhile owned-territory fruit clusters or an unusually large connected owned fruit network.

3. Natural-food worker protection
   - Civilians actively gathering/returning from live non-Field, non-Animal food supplies are excluded from Field crews.
   - Field actions wait for eligible newly trained future farmers instead of stealing existing food workers or farmers.
   - The existing required-builder/FARM_LOCK handoff keeps the selected builders working the completed Field.

4. First Barracks locality
   - Barracks #1 anchors to the completed Storehouse closest to the active wood worksite.
   - Remote frontier-resource candidates are skipped for Barracks #1.

5. Civic Centre civilian priority
   - The real 60-civilian ceiling, not a doctrine soft cap, controls the normal switch to CC military production.
   - Before 60, only real hostile defense or a live Phase-1 rush/attack permits CC soldiers.
   - Generic active P2/P3 and near-launch attack exceptions were removed.

OUT OF SCOPE / PRESERVED
- IT15.8 economic-safety kernel, attack execution, phase logic, army composition, production cycles, resource forecasts, and general building policy.
- No change to the +30% multiplier or historical benchmark results.
