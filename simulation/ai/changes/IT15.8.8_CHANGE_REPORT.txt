PETRA EXPERT IT15.8.8 — STAGED FORESTS AND OPENING PASS-THROUGH

Baseline
- Built directly on corrected IT15.8.7. Farmstead-builder food ownership, natural-food service rules, Barracks priority, and civilian-only CC production remain unchanged.

Corrections
- Opening wood candidates now measure both the immediately serviceable edge and the connected forest behind it.
- The score includes deeper continuation wood within a bounded reach, so a 1,100-wood edge into a 2,100+ forest can beat an isolated 1,100-wood pocket.
- Later wood-district checks use the same full-forest amount; a thin newly exposed edge is not rejected as a fragment when it opens a worthwhile deeper Storehouse progression.
- Diagnostics report immediate wood, full connected forest wood, and continuation wood separately.
- Up to four opening builders may pass through the Civic Center during the first 75 seconds when their straight route crosses the CC and their destination lies on the opposite side.
- Pass-through is limited to assigned construction workers, is one-use per worker, and logs garrison/unload events for replay verification.

Replay targets
- Prefer the Farmstead-side 2,100+ staged forest when its total continuation value exceeds a superficially similar isolated edge.
- Cut the edge, then allow a deeper Storehouse to advance into that same connected forest instead of ignoring it all game.
- Opening builders may save walking through the CC, but ordinary gatherers and later army movement remain untouched.
