EXPERT AI PETRA — IT14.99 CHANGE REPORT
=======================================

PURPOSE
IT14.99 is a focused combat-conversion build based on the first two IT14.98 tests.
IT14.98 proved that Expert can sustain/rebuild a much stronger economy, but Game 1
showed that poor fight conversion can still burn that advantage away. Game 2 showed
that the new Late-P1 mass timing can work extremely well. 14.99 therefore preserves
the 14.98 civilian/food/wood conversion architecture and fixes four observed issues.

1) FARMSTEAD FUTURE-RING QUALITY
- Farmstead scoring now strongly demotes sites with fewer than four FUTURE usable
  field positions after natural food disappears.
- This is a score, not a hard veto, so constrained maps cannot deadlock.
- Natural-expansion preference is raised from 3 to 4 future field slots.
- Goal: avoid a Farmstead whose one side is permanently blocked by an existing
  House/Barracks/other structure and can therefore support only 2-3 touching Fields.

2) EARLY-P1 DEADLINE MASS FALLBACK
- The preferred P1 tech package remains desirable.
- During the final 25 seconds of the Early-P1 opportunity window, an army that has
  reached the intended rush mass may launch without that package if the normal local
  defender/known-army/population safety checks are favorable.
- This fixes IT14.98 Game 1 cancelling 22 soldiers against only ~10 local defenders
  solely because package=missing.

3) HARD ATTRITION BUDGET
- IT14.94 local-dominance pressure hold remains useful for short tactical superiority.
- It no longer permits unlimited replacement feeding.
- A normal P2/P3 attack that has lost at least 36 units AND about 75% of its original
  launch size while enemy-population damage is below 50% of those losses exhausts its
  casualty budget and strategically rebooms, unless meaningful CC capture commitment
  applies.
- New diagnostic: [EXPERT-ATTRITION] budget-exhausted ...

4) ATHENS RICH-ECONOMY ELITE CORE
- Gymnasium composition is now role-based rather than a tiny generic 6/8 champion cap.
- Rich P2 target: 12 champion hoplites + 5 champion javelineers.
- Rich P3 target: 15 champion hoplites + 5 champion javelineers.
- Counts use live + AI-queued + currently-training units, so elite casualties reopen
  only their missing elite role. Normal citizen-soldier casualties continue through
  normal CC/Barracks replacement.
- Champion hoplites have first claim; champion javelineers are the second layer.
- Champion crossbows/Gastraphetes are no longer deliberately produced by the elite
  maintainer, and the generic AttackPlan crossbow-champion cap is 0.
- Elite production requires a genuinely rich bank and preserves a reserve so it does
  not starve ordinary reinforcement/eco continuity.
- New diagnostic: [EXPERT-ELITE] maintain role=hoplite|javelineer ...

FROZEN / INTENTIONALLY UNCHANGED
- 65 global civilian ceiling.
- Late-P1 48 civilian target / 55 efficiency recovery band.
- Late-P1 64 preferred / 52 minimum launch mass and P2 timing hold.
- Opening Storehouse selector/service logic from IT14.97.
- Opening Farmstead berry-cluster doctrine remains; only candidate ring-quality scoring
  is strengthened.
- Benchmark Top 3 remains canonical Athens Expert vs Athens Very Hard.

TEST FOCUS
- Farmsteads should usually preserve four usable sides for future Fields.
- Early-P1 should no longer cancel a favorable full-mass timing only for missing tech.
- Watch casualty exchange diagnostics; repeated 100+ loss / ~25 enemy-damage feeds
  should terminate much earlier.
- With a rich Town/City bank, expect [EXPERT-ELITE] to build/rebuild the champion
  hoplite core before premium ranged specialists.
