1) OPENING STOREHOUSE — BEST SERVICEABLE FOREST
14.96 still fed the IT14.93 selector only trees already inside our territory. On sparse maps that let a weak 300-500 wood CC clump win, then the later wood system discovered the better border forest and paid for Storehouse #2 almost immediately.

14.97:
- opening scan includes same-land neutral wood within the 180m opening search;
- a forest is eligible only if at least one nearby Storehouse position is in our own territory and on the same land access;
- sustainable opening forest floor = 800 wood when such a candidate exists; smaller clumps are bridge-only;
- legal Storehouse offset is included in scoring so a remote neutral forest cannot win merely because its resource center is rich;
- a materially richer sustainable forest can dominate a closer weaker one when the extra one-time builder walk is reasonable;
- opening Storehouse candidate ring is broad enough on the FIRST attempt to reach the legal edge of a neutral-border forest;
- diagnostic now reports mode, total/own/neutral wood, approach, legal offset and average drop distance.

Expected example:
  [EXPERT-WOOD-OPEN] selected mode=sustainable wood=1500 own=400 neutral=1100 approach=95.0 legalOffset=16.0 ...

2) BORDER WOOD IS ACTUALLY SERVICEABLE AFTER THE BUILD
A Storehouse legally built inside our territory can now service same-land neutral trees inside its local wood radius. The current district-health/cutting-front logic also counts those neutral continuation trees. This prevents the AI from selecting a border forest correctly and then immediately pretending the neutral half of that same forest does not exist.
