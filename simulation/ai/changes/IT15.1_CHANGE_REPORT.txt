CWA Petra Expert AI — IT15.1 Change Report
===========================================
Baseline: exact IT15.0 package supplied/tested in this project.
Scope: repair the IT15.0 food-backbone regression and enforce strict owned-territory gathering.

Why IT15.1 exists
-----------------
IT15.0 correctly lowered rush civilian counts, but it accidentally created a circular Field-building deadlock:
- Field BUILD actions required a special pool of newly trained "future farmer" civilians.
- Once those civilians completed Fields they became FARM_LOCKed and were excluded from later Field crews.
- Rush civilian production then stopped near its cap, so later Field actions logged `wait new-civilians=0/2` and were discarded.
- Food-owned civilians with no Field slot were then sent to wood/minerals as a productive fallback.
The result was the opposite of the Steven-derived labor model: too few Fields and too many dedicated civilians on secondary resources.

Binding IT15.1 changes
----------------------
1. FIELD ACTIONS MAY NOT DISAPPEAR FOR LACK OF NEW CIVILIANS
   - Existing farm/food civilians may be reused to construct the next Field.
   - FARM_LOCK no longer disqualifies an established farmer from a Field construction crew.
   - If the special future-farmer pool is still insufficient, the Field action remains alive and uses existing farm/food workers.
   - New diagnostic: `[EXPERT-FARM-CREW] reuse-existing-food ...`.

2. RUSH CIVILIANS ARE THE FOOD BACKBONE
   - In Early/Late P1 rush doctrine, only the three scripted opening civilian lumberjacks may remain dedicated to wood before six Fields exist.
   - Every other dedicated civilian is forcibly food-owned even if an older decision tick gave it a "permanent" wood/stone/metal job.
   - At six Fields, those three opening lumber civilians also migrate to food; citizen-soldiers own the secondary-resource economy.
   - A food-owned civilian with no current food slot is NOT redirected to wood/stone/metal. Missing food capacity is infrastructure debt.
   - Any food/farm civilian may help a compatible Field foundation, not just a specially tagged future farmer.
   - Food-home locality now means food: natural food, Fields, and food infrastructure. It no longer means "local wood/mineral fallback".

3. EARLIER PERMANENT FOOD FOR RUSH DOCTRINES
   - Rush natural-food transition begins at 70% remaining instead of waiting for the global 40% threshold.
   - Six-Field population floor moves from 45 -> 40 population.
   - Eight-Field population floor moves from 75 -> 70 population.
   - Natural-first logic may no longer erase a strategic 6/8-Field floor once that floor is active.

4. HARD TERRITORY SOVEREIGNTY
   - Expert economic workers may gather only resources whose MAP POSITION is inside PlayerID territory.
   - Neutral resources are no longer counted as immediately accessible stock by the economic forecast.
   - Neutral-border wood is no longer legal opening wood.
   - Neutral wood is removed from operational woodsite, cutting-front, district-health and rollover collectors.
   - The old emergency neutral-wood bridge is removed.
   - Neutral natural-food annexing is disabled: no Farmstead/worker may directly service neutral fruit/berries.
   - Hunting/chickens are territory-gated as well.
   - Runtime stale-target guard revokes any economic SUPPLY_ID that is outside PlayerID territory.
   - Neutral resources may still inform territorial-expansion logic; they are not gather targets until ownership changes.

5. FIELDS BEFORE SPECULATIVE DROPSITES
   - While permanent Field capacity is behind target and food is critical/short (or natural food is gone), proactive mixed/mineral Storehouses are suppressed.
   - Reactive stone/metal service Storehouses are also suppressed under the same food-capacity debt.
   - Dedicated wood rollover remains allowed so Expert can build the Storehouse actually needed to reach a legal owned forest.

Frozen / intentionally unchanged
--------------------------------
- Rush army launch package, target sizes and combat timing logic from IT15.0.
- IT14.99/15.0 rich-economy champion composition logic.
- Benchmark methodology and EXPERT_BENCHMARKS.txt.
- The anti-14.86 principle remains: Expert may open a NEW OWNED wood district when the current one is depleted/unusable. The new rule forbids neutral gathering; it does not restore the old "never open another woodline" veto.

Test focus
----------
For Early/Late P1 rush tests, inspect 4:00-10:00:
- At ~40 pop, the permanent target should be at least 6 Fields; at ~70 pop at least 8.
- `[EXPERT-FARM-CREW] wait new-civilians=...` should never appear.
- If the special crew is empty, expect `[EXPERT-FARM-CREW] reuse-existing-food ...` and the Field should still proceed.
- Food-owned civilians should not be sent to wood/minerals because a Field is missing.
- No worker should gather a resource outside owned territory; `[EXPERT-TERRITORY] revoke ...` is a bug-defense diagnostic if a stale target ever appears.
- Storehouse count should no longer race ahead of Fields simply to service metal/stone while food capacity is behind.
