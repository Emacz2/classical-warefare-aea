CWA Petra Expert AI — IT15.2 Change Report
==========================================

Purpose
-------
IT15.2 is a surgical correction to IT15.1's food-capacity/no-idle regression.

Replay failure
--------------
In the first IT15.1 test, food-owned civilians were intentionally parked in
AWAIT_FOOD_INFRASTRUCTURE when the natural-food network was saturated and no
Field was yet available. Idle workers rose into double digits even though legal
owned-territory productive work existed. At the same time, the Field watchdog
could wait for its ordinary delay/runway conditions instead of treating the
idle food workers as proof that capacity was already late.

Binding changes
---------------
1. NO IDLE FOOD CAPACITY WAIT:
   - Food-owned civilians never intentionally stand idle waiting for a Field.
   - If no natural/farm/build target exists, they take temporary useful work on
     owned-territory wood/stone/metal while retaining JOB_METADATA=food_owned.

2. IDLE FOOD WORKERS FORCE FIELDS:
   - Idle food-owned civilians immediately raise the Field watchdog target.
   - Watchdog delay is zero while food-owned idle capacity debt exists.
   - The Field action is inserted after opening-tech filtering, so Iron Axe
     preference cannot suppress a Field needed to stop actual worker idling.

3. TEMPORARY WORK IS NOT A ROLE CHANGE:
   - Temporary fallback does not convert the civilian into a permanent lumberjack.
   - Early/Late-P1 rush civilians retry food every Expert update.
   - A 30-second temporary wood lease may not delay return to newly available food.

4. TERRITORY RULE PRESERVED:
   - IT15.1's owned-territory-only gathering rule remains unchanged.
   - Temporary productive fallback is also restricted to owned territory.

Frozen
------
- Rush timing/combat package.
- IT15.1 territory sovereignty.
- 34/36 rush civilian doctrine and food ownership.
- Champion/combat changes inherited from IT14.99/15.0.
- Benchmark file.
