IT15.8.15 CHANGE REPORT

BASE
IT15.8.15 is a small corrective build directly on IT15.8.14. It does not replace
the opening placement, worker-ownership, Field-crew, expansion, technology, or army
motion work that already passed.

REPLAY EVIDENCE — 20260917-131122
- Field #1 could not be authorized until 3:59 because the combined-natural-food hold
  remained active above 25%.
- Dedicated civilian lumber reached 20 while natural food was still active, then
  continued toward 27 because policy allowed 28.
- House #2 began around 30/40 population before any Field existed.
- Civilian batches could never exceed four.
- Field #7 was well placed but arrived around 8:00, after the food-starved P2 transition.
- The P3 Boom attack/cleanup package converted its winning position too slowly.

CORRECTIONS
1. Permanent-food transition begins at 40% combined in-territory natural food. The
   last natural food now overlaps construction of Fields by newly trained civilians.
2. Dedicated civilian lumber is capped at 20. Citizen-soldiers and genuinely temporary
   overflow may still supply wood; new civilians are reserved for food growth.
3. House #2 waits when real population has more than five free slots and no Field has
   started. Population emergencies remain authoritative.
4. Civilian training keeps three-unit opening batches, then uses four at 350 food and
   five at 700 food when population/civilian headroom permits.
5. P3 support Markets require 90m separation and prefer 135m rather than clustering.
6. P3 Boom's combat threshold is reduced from 90 to 84 plus its home reserve, with
   hard/absolute release moved to 15:30/17:00. It still requires real siege and the
   existing readiness gates.
7. At enemy population 16 or lower, siege executes the enemy Civic Centre instead of
   extending a capture/cleanup loop until population eight.

UNCHANGED
- CC trains no ordinary soldiers before the civilian target except a P1 attack or
  genuine defense exception.
- Natural-food workers are not pulled to build Fields; new civilians own the farm handoff.
- Barracks #2 still uses six completed Fields as its launch floor, while the economy
  continues immediately toward eight and then measured demand.
- IT15.8.14 Town eco-tech, expansion, and launched-army recovery rules remain binding.
