CWA Petra Expert AI — IT15.5 Change Report

STATUS
IT15.4 is treated as a failed regression build. IT15.5 is a surgical correction of the
three failure modes demonstrated in the Athens Expert-vs-Very-Hard Savanna replay.

1. WOOD MIGRATION CAN NEVER CREATE IDLE SOLDIERS
- IT15.4 staged exhausted woodsite releases, but workers beyond the packet cap could
  receive wood-migration-hold while already idle.
- IT15.5 permits a migration hold only when the worker still has a live wood gather
  order or is returning a wood load.
- If that worker becomes idle, the hold is invalidated immediately and the worker is
  released to productive work, even if this temporarily exceeds the preferred packet.
- Productive old cutters are still protected from unnecessary mass migration.

2. GAIA / WILDLIFE IS NOT A MILITARY THREAT
- The IT15.4 Savanna replay strongly indicated that elephants were entering the Expert
  strategic defense scan, mobilizing citizen-soldiers and even an emergency tower.
- scanIncomingBaseThreat now explicitly excludes owner 0 and any unit whose owner is
  not an actual enemy player.
- Wildlife may never trigger Expert strategic mobilization.

3. P1 RUSH GATE REBALANCED
- IT15.4 over-priced hypothetical reinforcement and production, turning an Early P1
  Rush into a no-rush game.
- Reinforcement awareness remains, but the radius and weights are reduced:
    nearby mobile weight 0.50 (was 0.75)
    distant reserve weight 0.10 (was 0.25)
    production equivalent 1 (was 2)
    reinforcement radius 155m (was 190m)
    production radius 145m (was 175m)
- Early-P1 global known-army requirement is capped at rush target mass + 8.
- The separate P1 timing-window global requirement is capped at 32.
- Local defenders, nearby reinforcements, static defenses, and enemy population still
  veto genuinely suicidal attacks.

FROZEN / PRESERVED
- IT15.3 Storehouse forecast crash fix.
- IT15.3 failed-rush retreat/abort behavior.
- IT15.2 food-capacity no-idle contract and owned-territory sovereignty.
- Barracks batch training, P3 parallel Forge work, Iphicrates support behavior.
- Canonical benchmark leaderboard/methodology unchanged except active test-build note.
