EXPERT AI PETRA — IT14.91 CHANGE REPORT
=======================================

PURPOSE
IT14.91 is the first four-resource forecast/replan pass. The selected Expert doctrine
still defines what the AI wants to buy, but food/wood/stone/metal allocation now reacts
to the map and to projected spending instead of relying mainly on isolated bank thresholds.

FILES CHANGED FROM IT14.90
- petra/expertDecisionController.js
- petra/expertDecision/policy.js
- petra/expertDecision/economyPlanner.js
- petra/expertDecision/resourceForecast.js   [NEW]
- EXPERT_BENCHMARKS.txt
- IT14.91_CHANGE_REPORT.txt                  [NEW]

attackManager.js and attackPlan.js are unchanged from IT14.90.

1) FOUR-RESOURCE FORECAST GOVERNOR
- New pure resourceForecast.js evaluates food, wood, stone and metal on the same basis:
    current bank
  + measured delivered income
  + immediately accessible own/near-neutral supply
  versus
    queued costs
  + phase/doctrine projected spending
  + a modest working reserve.
- Uses short (~60s) and medium (~150s) horizons plus dynamic float ceilings.
- Own "immediately accessible" supply is limited to the current dropsite/CC economic
  footprint; remote owned resources no longer make the forecast falsely comfortable.
- Bursty phase/tech purchases are smoothed/clamped so one purchase does not look like
  a permanent enormous burn rate.
- New diagnostic:
  [EXPERT-FORECAST] target=... donor=... F=bank/income/access/demand/margin:status ...

2) WORKER REPLANNING / FLOAT CONTROL
- Existing worker rebalancing now consumes the forecast directive when available.
- Critical shortages may move up to 10 workers on a short cooldown; normal corrections
  are smaller/slower.
- Large stone/metal/wood/food floats become donor resources. Workers can be peeled from
  a resource whose future coverage is excessive toward the least-covered future demand.
- Several older fixed resource rebalance passes are suppressed while the forecast is
  active so competing rules do not immediately undo the new allocation.
- Permanent Field-locked civilians remain protected.
- IT14.90 future-farmer ownership remains unchanged.

3) MAP ADAPTATION / BRIDGE WOOD / EXPANSION
- A forecast-critical wood shortage may use small ~120+ wood palm/tree fragments as
  temporary bridge wood when immediately accessible wood is <= ~650.
- The normal 600-wood fragment rejection remains on healthy maps.
- Scarcity expansion can now be triggered by a forecast-proven shortage of ANY resource,
  not only legacy wood thresholds.
- A large bank can suppress a false "wood income stalled" crisis when projected wood
  demand is already safely covered.
- Athens may consider a rich Cleruchy frontier from ~3:30 / 40 pop during a genuine
  forecast-critical P1 supply crisis; the missing resource gets extra frontier weight.
  Normal Cleruchy timing remains unchanged on healthy maps.

4) NATURAL FOOD OUTRANKS A NEW FIELD
- Safe same-land neutral fruit/berry clusters near the border are visible to the forecast.
- A worthwhile neutral natural-food cluster can PREEMPT a Field build this frame.
- The Farmstead is still required to resolve to a legal owned-territory position.
- Natural-food Farmsteads now have a separate cap (5) from the permanent farm-hub cap (3),
  so a third permanent farm hub cannot make later valuable natural food invisible.
- New diagnostic:
  [EXPERT-FORECAST] natural-food PREEMPT ...

5) PROACTIVE MIXED STOREHOUSE SERVICE
- Storehouse planning may now score wood + stone + metal together around the actual
  economic footprint instead of waiting for several workers to suffer a long haul first.
- A worked metal/stone deposit plus nearby wood can justify one mixed Storehouse.
- Candidate value includes resource mass, worker count, resource-type coverage and
  average haul-distance improvement.
- This path does NOT switch primary wood districts. A healthy woodline remains the
  current lumber district unless the dedicated wood rollover says otherwise.
- Pure healthy-wood proactive camps are still left to the wood-district system.
- New diagnostic:
  [EXPERT-SERVICE] build=storehouse resource=mixed ...

6) FORECAST-AWARE BARTER
- In P2+, if a real shortage exists while another resource is far above its dynamic
  future-use ceiling, an existing Market may sell up to ~500 of the donor resource.
- A Market can be requested for a severe forecast shortage + giant surplus even when
  the old food-only recovery test is not active.
- New diagnostic:
  [EXPERT-FORECAST] barter sell=... buy=...

FROZEN / NON-REGRESSION
- IT14.86 opening Farmstead multi-bush behavior.
- IT14.87 practical wood district + emergency escape and P3 FINISH persistence.
- IT14.88 no automatic bolt shooters, healer escort, optional Iphicratean Reforms.
- IT14.89 Storehouse placement scoring, Farmstead #3 rescan, Scythian Police max-pop gate,
  house-gap cleanup and Field progress watchdog.
- IT14.90 permanent future-farmer crews: normally 3-4 newly trained civilians build the
  Field and those same civilians stay farming.
- IT14.90 bounded manager attack-move and decisive CC capture finish lock.
- attackManager.js and attackPlan.js unchanged.

OFFLINE VALIDATION

- Synthetic Arctic giant-float forecast: wood/stone are classified surplus rather than
  treating zero current woodcutters alone as a wood crisis.
- All Petra JavaScript files must pass ordinary Node syntax and ES-module parse checks
  before packaging.
