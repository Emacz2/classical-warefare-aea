Expert AI Petra — IT14.68 Test Report
=====================================

Baseline
--------
IT14.68 is built from the cleaned IT14.67 package. The Expert gather/trade
multiplier remains frozen at 1.35. This release is a stability/execution pass,
not a broad strategy rewrite.

Why IT14.68 exists: the three IT14.67 replays
---------------------------------------------
1. Replay 1 — BARE PASS, 24:41.2.
   The main army eventually won, but P2 Tech Push waited too long, the Civic
   Centre had production gaps, some permanent fields drifted away from their
   Farmstead, and the AI spent a winning Town position on City/Prytaneion
   instead of immediately producing Town-legal rams.

2. Replay 2 — FAIL / user stopped at 12:51.8.
   The opening natural-food play was good, but the handoff failed. Expert had
   two Barracks and reached 70 civilians, yet remained in Village with only
   three fields. A legal fourth-field ring still existed, active wood gathering
   collapsed to zero, military production kept consuming the trickle, and a
   defensive mobilization later released a 70-unit reserve with no P1 attack
   path. Telemetry reached 62 idle units.

3. Replay 3 — WIN, 19:40.4.
   Early P1 timing succeeded, but the win still exposed severe trainer gaps.
   Barracks gaps in the current session included 49.6s, 91.2s, 217.6s,
   233.6s and 235.2s. The Civic Centre also stopped producing at the doctrine
   civilian soft cap instead of immediately becoming another military trainer.

IT14.68 changes
---------------
1. Production continuity contract
   - Every completed Barracks maintains current + next Expert military work
     after the protected opening pulse.
   - The Civic Centre buffers civilians up to the current doctrine civilian cap.
   - Once that current civilian cap is reached (including a doctrine soft cap),
     the Civic Centre immediately becomes a current + next military trainer.
   - At/near maximum population, one-unit standby replacement orders remain
     queued rather than leaving trainers empty.
   - Standby military orders are lower priority than phase-critical fields,
     phase research and dedicated siege.
   - When siege needs population room, ordinary standby soldier orders are
     pruned before ram production.

2. Four-field P2 phase insurance
   - From ~5:30 in Village, once two Barracks exist, a P2 transition below the
     absolute six-field two-Barracks floor receives a hard high-priority field action.
   - If no touching field slot exists, the high-priority action becomes another
     Farmstead, not a remote field.
   - Existing lower-priority field/Farmstead actions are promoted rather than
     duplicated.
   - The phase-safety pipeline counts a future field once only; active task +
     foundation are not double-counted.
   - This is insurance only: strong natural food may still delay ordinary farm
     expansion and remains the preferred early food source.

3. Faster real wood-collapse detection
   - If the economy wants a meaningful wood workforce but actual wood gatherers
     remain zero for roughly eight seconds, the wood-stall watchdog fires even
     when stale recent deliveries would previously have masked the collapse.

4. No stranded giant P1 army
   - A Village-phase reserve of roughly 45+ combat units after ~6:00 can open a
     Default/Huge fallback attack path even under the P2 Tech Push doctrine.
   - The fallback still uses the existing live strength/timing gate; it is not a
     blind attack command.
   - A defense army released after the threat disappears can therefore become
     offensive pressure instead of returning 60-70 troops to idle reserve.

5. P2 Tech Push opportunity override
   - The normal 45+ opportunity still prefers at least one active core military
     technology.
   - At 60+ troops, Expert may take a favorable opportunity with zero active
     core techs rather than parking a huge army indefinitely. The same enemy
     strength gate must still approve the fight.

6. Town-phase kill switch / siege conversion
   - A healthy started Town attack with roughly 45+ troops can enter kill mode
     when the opponent is <=70 population or Expert has a >=35 population lead,
     provided the visible enemy combat force is small enough and the escort
     ratio remains safe.
   - Kill mode suppresses/cancels City research and long-payback Town support
     spending, builds the Town-legal Arsenal/Siege Workshop path, and targets
     two rams by default or three against multiple hard targets.
   - Rams receive a dedicated higher-priority queue before ordinary infantry.

7. Hard-touch permanent farms
   - Permanent field placement is capped to an approximately 0.8m border gap.
   - Repeated placement failures no longer widen the ring to 1.3m, 2m, 4m,
     10m or 18m.
   - Terrain pressure is solved by another Farmstead, preserving the intended
     visual and economic district layout.

Preserved behavior
------------------
- Expert multiplier stays 1.35.
- The successful IT14.66/14.67 timing-strength logic remains in place.
- Natural-food sequential expansion remains enabled; IT14.68 does not force
  early farms merely because Replay 2 later deadlocked.
- Existing bad-exchange/reboom logic, champion caps, premium reinforcement,
  barter correction, true-siege classification and 2-3 ram finishing doctrine
  remain intact unless explicitly superseded above.

Validation target
-----------------
The next test set should judge variance as much as average speed. A single fast
win is not enough: we want the three doctrines/maps to stop producing one
19-minute win beside a 60-idle-unit deadlock. Production continuity, phase
handoff and winning-position conversion should now be deterministic floors.

REVISED 14.68 P2 FOOD FLOOR
---------------------------
- With two completed Barracks, all normal P2 readiness lanes now require a six-field
  pipeline. Healthy natural food may still be harvested and can delay extra farms, but
  it no longer permits a two-Barracks phase-up below six permanent fields.
- The 5:30 phase-safety action therefore drives 3/4/5 fields to 6 before normal P2.
- The only bypass is the existing genuine food-geometry deadlock escape around 9:00.
- A future one-Barracks fast-P2 doctrine can use a separate four-field floor; no such
  doctrine is introduced in this revision.
