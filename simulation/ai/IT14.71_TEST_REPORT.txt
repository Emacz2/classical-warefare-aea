EXPERT AI PETRA — IT14.71 TEST REPORT
=====================================

Purpose
-------
IT14.71 is an execution-efficiency pass built directly from the tested IT14.70 package.
The gather/trade multiplier remains 1.35. The strong IT14.70 attack/P2/siege behavior is
left alone; this revision targets recurring small mechanical inefficiencies seen across
the AI replays and the partial Steven human benchmark.

Primary changes
---------------
1. Four-worker Field construction -> immediate farming
   - Standard Field desired builders: 4 (was 1).
   - Every live Field foundation is topped up toward four food civilians.
   - Their resource ownership is unchanged while building.
   - On completion, those same builders are permanently locked to the Field and receive
     the gather order immediately.

2. Civilian resource permanence
   - Established civilians no longer cross food/wood/stone/metal because of normal bank
     balancing or a 30-second lease expiring.
   - Construction is a temporary pause, not a job change.
   - Cross-resource civilian moves are reserved for genuine food/wood emergency banks.
   - Citizen-soldiers remain the flexible balancing pool.

3. Food-first early surplus
   - Food priority bank raised from 700 to 1000.
   - New-civilian wood-surplus redirect delayed from 1200 food to 1800 food.
   - Generic mining waits for 6 completed Fields (was 4), those completed Field slots
     are filled first, ~45 civilians (was 35), and a real 900-food bank (was 750).
   - Fixed an older wiring bug where miningFoodFloor was passed into the civilian
     policy but never actually used by it.
   - Normal farmer->wood release is disabled; only the extreme wood-starvation escape
     may break a preferred Field crew.

4. Better permanent Farmstead quality
   - Dedicated permanent farm hubs may not degrade below 3 legal hard-touch field slots.
   - Natural-food Farmsteads remain the exception because their primary purpose is
     servicing berries/fruit.
   - The existing one-Barracks/four-field P2 escape remains the anti-deadlock mechanism.

5. Housing cleanup
   - Once Home Garden is committed at house 12, ordinary House planning is suppressed.
   - At/after the mandatory threshold, all normal House queue entries are removed, not
     only Expert-tagged ones, preventing generic Petra from sneaking house 14 behind the
     Expert housing-tech plan.

Preserved contracts
-------------------
- 1.35 Expert gather/trade multiplier.
- Hard-touch Field geometry <=0.8m from Farmstead.
- Normal six-field/two-Barracks P2 doctrine and one-Barracks/four-field deadlock escape.
- Unified timing-strength attack gate.
- P2 mass opportunity, kill switch, coherent reinforcement, production continuity.
- Wood Level-2 recovery.
- Two-ram normal finishing package.
- Champion limits and other IT14.70 combat rules.

Validation targets
------------------
- A new Field should rapidly show ~4 committed builders, then the same crew should become
  its first permanent farmers immediately after completion.
- Established civilian resource jobs should remain stable through ordinary bank changes.
- Early food should generally lead secondary resources rather than repeatedly collapsing
  below wood/stone/metal.
- Dedicated Farmsteads should normally support 3-4 Fields; a 0-2 Field Farmstead should
  be explainable as a natural-food dropsite, not a permanent farm hub.
- No ordinary house 14 after Home Garden is committed.
