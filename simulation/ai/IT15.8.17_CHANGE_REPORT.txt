IT15.8.17 — OPENING WORKSITE / SPARSE-BIOME CORRECTION

Scope
-----
This is a narrow correction to IT15.8.16. It does not change field demand,
general civilian allocation, doctrine selection, or attack grouping.

Changes
-------
1. House #1 now commits at 24/30 accounted population (six free slots).
2. House #1 searches beside the active opening wood/storehouse district before
   using the old CC-oriented fallback ring.
3. Exactly four opening citizen-soldier woodworkers build House #1. Natural-food
   civilians and newly trained civilians are excluded from its builder pool.
4. Sparse opening forests (<=1,200 local wood) may trigger a replacement
   Storehouse handoff after Barracks #1, even when the lumber crew is too small
   to satisfy the old large-crew handoff rule.
5. Rich safe hunt can lower the protected food reserve enough to batch the two
   additional pursuit hunters together when both are genuinely affordable.
6. Hunt diagnostics now print the actual requested batch size.
7. For Athens, Sparta, and Thebes, a P1 rush still assembling after 6:00 becomes
   a Forge + Melee Attack I timing package. One completed Barracks is sufficient
   to begin that package, and Hoplite Tradition no longer substitutes for the
   attack upgrade once the rush has become delayed.

Preserved contracts
-------------------
- CC remains civilian-only through the protected opening window.
- No second Storehouse/Farmstead before Barracks #1.
- Existing natural-food ownership, Wicker handoff, field placement, farm demand,
  general worker balancing, and compact later-house behavior are unchanged.
- A true early P1 launch may still prioritize speed instead of Melee Attack I.
- EXPERT_BENCHMARKS is unchanged pending a clean replay.
