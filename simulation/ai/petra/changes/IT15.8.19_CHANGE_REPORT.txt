IT15.8.19 — P1 PACKAGE / COMBAT CONTACT CORRECTION

Replay findings
---------------
- Replay 1 rolled early_p1_rush. Metal-cost Hoplites consumed the starting metal,
  leaving Forge=false and melee=false through the P1 deadline.
- The expired rush later converted to p2_tech_push and used the old >=60-army
  no-tech opportunity escape, producing the observed unupgraded 14:15 attack.
- Replay 2 rolled late_p1_rush. Its outskirts grapes were neutral by the territory
  raster even though the cluster visibly touched the border.

Corrections
-----------
1. Forge construction no longer waits for simultaneous affordability of Forge,
   Melee-I, and every operating reserve.
2. Once the Greek P1 Forge window opens, soldier selection protects the real live
   Forge + Melee-I costs. If Hoplites would consume protected metal, the Barracks
   selects an affordable ranged alternative such as a javelineer; the no-idle
   fallback cannot steal the protected package.
3. A non-finishing P2 attack cannot use army size as a substitute for military
   research. At least one relevant upgrade must be completed, researching, or queued.
4. An army entering CC/tower/fortress fire redirects every eligible combat unit into
   attack-move contact, including structures, instead of leaving the formation idle.
5. Opening House #1 must be 10–20m from the opening Storehouse and remain on its
   protected CC-facing side, away from the forest/deeper Storehouse path.
6. Natural food within 25m of owned territory is recognized as frontier food.
   Normal construction and worker legality still apply.
7. Frontier wood within 25m is visible to Storehouse/expansion planning while direct
   lumber orders remain restricted to owned territory.
8. The strategic mineral floor now remains active after the forecast governor starts.
   P2/P3 builds establish metal and stone before phase, Hoplite and Forge demand stalls;
   civilians join mining after the food/wood opening and a bounded number of citizen
   soldiers may reinforce it without stripping farms.
9. Routine Forge construction is capped at two. A third is disabled unless explicitly
   re-enabled with two occupied research lanes and an immediately fundable backlog.
10. A P3 boom may convert a clear advantage from 13:00 with 84 army, real siege, two
    completed military technologies, and enemy population no more than 75% of ours.
