Corrections
1. Sparse-biome wood service
   - When wood is critical and the bank is <= 200, nearby legal fragments within 75m
     are evaluated as one economic district.
   - A combined 350+ wood district may place a Storehouse even when every connected
     sub-cluster fails the ordinary 500–600 wood threshold.
2. Farm spending gate
   - Farmer ownership no longer recursively demands another Field when the current
     field pipeline already covers live CC+barracks burn.
   - At 900+ food and <=250 wood, an established six-plus-field economy freezes new
     Field spending until the imbalance clears.
3. P3/CC spending ownership
   - P3 all-in commits the City phase through the majorTech queue at 12:00/100+ pop
     as soon as prerequisites expose the phase technology.
   - The phase queue receives priority 1125 so lower-priority spending cannot steal
     its reserved resources.
   - During that P3 spending window, Athens may buy Hoplite Tradition without waiting
     for both broad Town attack upgrades, preventing an otherwise idle CC.
4. Persistent enemy-CC capture
   - Once capture has gained at least 25 points and local overmatch remains valid, a
     temporary progress stall refreshes capture orders instead of switching to destroy.
   - The old destroy/retry fallback remains only for attempts that made no meaningful
     capture progress.
