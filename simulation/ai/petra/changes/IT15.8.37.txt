Expert AI IT15.8.37 — Preserve a won battlefield

Scope: two narrow corrections on top of the complete IT15.8.36 Petra folder.

1. Live military advantage now outranks stale cumulative attrition.
   - When an active army still has at least 40 units, at least 24 nearby combatants,
     no local static defenses, and an overwhelming local lead (at least 2:1 and +18),
     Expert keeps the current attack alive.
   - Earlier casualties are checkpointed at that moment. Only new casualties can
     contribute to a later bad-exchange retreat.
   - The broken-melee-screen rule cannot immediately cancel this same decisive hold.
   - Real retreats for local collapse, depleted armies, defended positions, and bad
     exchanges remain unchanged.
   - New replay marker: [EXPERT-ADVANTAGE] preserve.

2. City-phase ram selection prefers the fortified/city ram.
   - In Phase 3, automatic ram selection chooses units/*/siege_ram_city (or an
     equivalent City/Fortified ram) ahead of the cheaper basic ram.
   - Phase 2 behavior and optional non-ram siege selection remain unchanged.

Replay reason for the change:
The IT15.8.36 army reached approximately 83 local combatants versus 8 with zero local
defenses, but historical casualties triggered [EXPERT-ATTRITION] budget-exhausted and
[EXPERT-REBOOM]. That discarded a won battlefield and prolonged the match.
