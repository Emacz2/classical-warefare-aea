Expert Petra 15.8.34 patch — apply after 15.8.33

Scope: three corrective areas only. No gathering bonus, opening placement, farm,
Wicker, house, teleport, cavalry, technology-cost or difficulty changes.

1. Resource-district locality and woodline persistence
   - Resource-service Storehouses now carry the exact local worker IDs from the
     district audit into builder selection. The generic builder resolver can no
     longer pull workers from another productive woodline to construct them.
   - Forecast mixed/mineral Storehouses use workers already assigned to the
     serviced supplies whenever those workers exist.
   - Completion preserves those builders' original resource job, so constructing
     a mineral Storehouse does not silently convert its crew into lumberjacks.

2. Coherent P1 commitment and production
   - Wood scarcity may pivot an unformed P1 rush, but it cannot cancel a rush that
     has already reached its declared army target. The completed army is explicitly
     launch-authorized instead.
   - A legitimate earlier pivot immediately removes pending CC-combat orders and
     closes the P1 CC military exception; existing trained soldiers are retained.
   - While below the civilian target, an underfilled Barracks receives military
     resources before the CC. This favors a funded Barracks batch instead of
     splitting the same bank into one CC soldier and one Barracks soldier.

3. Decisive tower/CC finishing
   - Infantry-overmatch capture permission is reevaluated from the live army and
     enemy state for every started Expert plan. It no longer depends on whether
     the plan originally launched through the overmatch shortcut.
   - A no-siege overmatch clears an exposed tower first when necessary, then hard
     locks the enemy CC for capture. It does not wait for a ram.
   - New [EXPERT-CAPTURE-FINISH] LOCK diagnostics identify the no-siege execution.

Verification
   - node --check passes for expertDecisionController.js and attackPlan.js.
   - All six existing Petra regression scripts pass (nine PASS groups).
   - No engine simulation was run. Test two replays: abort the first if locality or
     strategy coherence fails; let the second reach conquest to measure finish time.
