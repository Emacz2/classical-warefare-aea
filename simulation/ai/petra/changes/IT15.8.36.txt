Expert AI IT15.8.36
====================

Scope
-----
Small corrective patch on top of IT15.8.35. It preserves the 15.8.35
AUTHORIZED -> STARTED attack handoff and the existing economy/opening rules.

Changes
-------
1. Committed-use gate for an additional farmstead
   - The farm-capacity deadlock path no longer purchases another farmstead
     unless at least two fields are still needed and enough wood exists to
     begin using the new hub.
   - An approved hub carries a debt of two or three fields.
   - Once the hub completes, those fields are requested specifically around
     that farmstead and the debt is reduced only as each field completes.
   - Existing farmstead slots are still rescanned before another hub is bought.

2. Decisive tower-to-CC attack handoff
   - A nearby exposed tower receives a bounded suppression squad (10-24 units,
     never more than 30% of the army), favoring melee units.
   - The rest of an overwhelming army is no longer redirected to the tower.
   - With at least 90 army units and enemy population no greater than 75% of
     that army, the plan directly executes the enemy CC instead of waiting for
     ram staging.
   - The established global rule that CC execution outranks tower suppression
     remains intact.

Validation
----------
- JavaScript syntax checks passed for all three Expert integration files.
- All nine existing regression test groups passed.
