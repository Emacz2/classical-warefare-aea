# Protected behavior and regression requirements

- Infantry citizen soldiers never gather food or farm. Cavalry hunt separately.
- Gather targets must be in owned territory, even during emergencies.
- Illegal work must attempt permitted productive work, not just erase metadata.
- Preserve opening berry dispersion independently of Wicker.
- Wicker requires additional local food, not one opening cluster alone.
- Preserve worksite/house placement and barracks timing while changing safety.
- Civilians maintain natural-food jobs until depleted; food transitions must work.
- Count walking/returning wood civilians toward the wood assignment target.
- CC soldier training before 60 civilians requires P1 rush or active attack defense.
- Adequate food must not automatically cause additional permanent farm hubs.
- Metal for hoplites must receive workers before production stalls.
- Resource workers should normally carry no farther than 25–35 m to a drop
  site; wood shortages or no legal placement can temporarily delay a new site.
- P2 tech push and P3 boom evaluate economic upgrades early while preserving
  enough resources for the next phase and essential food infrastructure.

These are requirements, not a claim that every item is implemented or game-tested.
Use STATUS.md and actual tests/replays to establish verification.
