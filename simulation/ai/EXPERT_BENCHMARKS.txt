EXPERT BENCHMARKS — IT14.94

PRIMARY GOAL
Make Expert harder in the areas the 2026-09-11 human-vs-Expert replay exposed—battlefield
judgment, proportional defense, repeated resource expansion and premium-infrastructure
placement—without regressing the IT14.93 opening/resource fixes.

FROZEN / HARD NON-REGRESSION
- Opening Storehouse must still choose the best sustainable forest, not a tiny near-CC clump.
- No immediate Storehouse #2 during the 45-second establishment grace.
- ZERO woodsite.averageDropDistance finite-number error spam.
- Keep IT14.92 forecast/actual-work economy authority.
- Keep future-farmer Field crews, natural-food priority, healer escort, no-bolt rule,
  Iphicratean optionality, house/farm geometry, CC civilian continuity and siege behavior.

1) P2 PRESSURE-HOLD TEST
When a normal P2/P3-nonterminal attack has a poor historical casualty exchange but still
owns the local battlefield:
- expect [EXPERT-PRESSURE] hold ...
- DO NOT expect [EXPERT-REBOOM] bad-exchange solely at readings resembling
  56v0 / 50v1 / 74v0 / 64v0 / 84v0 with a 40+ army;
- if static defenses are present, a short REGROUP is acceptable, but the same attack plan
  should survive and resume;
- if the army genuinely collapses/outnumbers locally, ordinary reboom MUST still happen.

2) DEFENSE TEST
For a raid of roughly 8-12 units while Expert has a large 70-100-man military:
- [EXPERT-DEF] mobilize should show a bounded response, not the entire army;
- home/reserve soldiers should answer before pulling the active offensive army;
- main attack pressure should continue where practical;
- a large/outmatching enemy attack may still mobilize everything;
- normal engage should not fire with 22 assembled against 36 unless the enemy is already
  inside the immediate-engage radius.

3) REPEAT EXPANSION TEST
Athens:
- first Cleruchy behavior remains valid;
- if later resource scarcity returns, a second and eventually third Cleruchy are legal;
- repeat expansion must be >=72m from an existing Cleruchy and respect the 90s completion
  cooldown;
- later frontier search may extend outward from a completed Cleruchy rather than staying
  trapped in the original CC ring;
- no more than one live Cleruchy build task/foundation at a time.

4) CLERUCHY PLACEMENT FALLBACK TEST
If a chosen frontier district cannot legally place:
- expect [EXPERT-EXPAND] blacklist cleruchy-anchor ...
- the next attempt should choose a distinct candidate, not repeat the same x/z forever;
- no repeated 6056-rejection loop against the same resource anchor;
- Cleruchy fallback must remain near useful frontier resources, not become a random
  home-base building.

5) ATHENS PREMIUM TRANSITION TEST
On a mature Town/City Athens economy with enough bank:
- if [EXPERT-ATHENS] build=gymnasium appears and the first search fails, the widened owned-
  territory grid should have a materially better chance to place it;
- once built, the pre-existing champion training doctrine should be free to operate;
- this patch does NOT require a new cavalry doctrine or force champions when unaffordable.

6) OPENING/ECONOMY SANITY CHECK
In the first 5-8 minutes compare against IT14.93:
- Storehouse #1 location and opening workers should look the same or better, not different
  because of 14.94;
- no new idle-opening regression;
- no new Farmstead/Field/house placement regression;
- [EXPERT-IT14.94] diagnostics must continue throughout the replay.
