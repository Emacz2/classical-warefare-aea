IT14.45 target
- Rotate <=25% health citizen-soldiers out of active attacks; once home they return to work while fresh >=75% health soldiers replace them.
- Preserve the full ~22-unit retreat/reboom as the fallback for a genuinely collapsed defended push.
- Active attack infantry may garrison rams. Ram passengers remember the attack plan and rejoin after unloading.
- While a ram is approaching the enemy CC, the bulk army fights on the perimeter and avoids diving under the CC/tower/fortress envelope. Once the ram reaches the assault radius, the army pivots inward with it.
- In P3, a healthy active attack can trigger one ram before formal finishing mode; finishing mode still targets up to two.
- After the first strategic placement failure, the second Town-support Market samples the actual owned territory grid for any legal phase-progress location.
- The second P2 food+wood eco pair is allowed after the first two military upgrades are in the pipeline instead of waiting for four military upgrades.
- Keep +35% gather/trade unchanged.

IT14.46 target
- Select one replay-deterministic Expert doctrine per match: Early P1 Rush (20%), Late P1 Timing Rush (25%), or P2 Forge-Tech Push (55%).
- Early/late P1 doctrines use temporary civilian soft caps (42/55) and earlier soldier/barracks timings so the rush is a real economic commitment; the cap returns to 70 after the timing window.
- P2 Forge-Tech Push keeps the 70-civilian boom and sweeps all currently available Village-phase ResourceGatherer technologies before/while Town Phase is researched, while preserving the phase cost until it is reserved.
- Start food/wood balance correction earlier and with smaller hysteretic batches so the ~5:00 food surplus does not become the ~8:00 wood surplus seen in IT14.45.
- Houses require less speculative headroom, need a larger true wood surplus for prebuild, and may not occupy the immediate storehouse worksite ring.
- Large 16+ worker forests can request a second same-patch dropsite before depletion; deepen-patch placement accepts a useful smaller distance gain and slightly tighter storehouse spacing.
- Smaller worthwhile natural-food branches may earn a farmstead before permanent fields; markets no longer count as permanent field hubs.
- Temple placement may use the CC economic core and is ranked primarily by live worker coverage inside its planning aura.
- Infantry-only Expert pressure avoids CC/tower/fortress strongpoints while the opponent still has a meaningful population; rushes seek undefended economic targets.
- At <=8 enemy population, finishing retargets visible ConquestCritical units first, then occupied garrison holders, then ConquestCritical structures. Rams use the same cleanup exception instead of repeatedly staging on a surviving CC.
- Keep +35% gather/trade unchanged; benchmark doctrine selection, BREAK, VICTORY, resource banks, P1 eco completion at Town transition, and last-target cleanup separately.
