EXPERT BENCHMARKS — IT14.89

IT14.88 VALIDATED / KEEP FROZEN
- Expert automatic siege should use rams / suitable catapult-style building siege, not Oxybeles/bolt shooters.
- Small healer escorts (about 2 Town / 3 City) should trail live attacks without counting toward launch army size.
- Iphicratean Reforms remains an optional Athens surplus tradeoff and may never delay the P3 all-in.
- IT14.87 P3 FINISH persistence and wood-crisis escape remain authoritative.

IT14.89 TARGETS
1) CONTACT FIGHTING / ATTACK-MOVE
- When an attacking army encounters enemy combat units on the route, infantry should engage/sweep those units before tunneling on nearby buildings.
- Watch for [EXPERT-ATTACK-MOVE] sweep diagnostics during real contact.
- Rams, healers/support, wounded peel units and tactical regroup must not be hijacked by this correction.
- attackPlan.js must remain unchanged.

2) WOOD DISTRICT DISCIPLINE
- If the current serviced forest still has >=~900 practical wood, >=4 real active cutters and healthy delivery, a few drifted workers must NOT open a second forest merely because their haul is long.
- If active cutters collapse, delivery stalls, or the fast <=250 wood / <=2-active crisis fires, release continuity immediately and recover/expand as IT14.87 did.
- A large single forest may still receive a second Storehouse when the cutting face has moved far enough for strong measured payback.

3) STOREHOUSE ECONOMIC PLACEMENT
- New wood Storehouses should sit on the working edge / weighted mass of the real forest, not beside one isolated fringe tree.
- Stone and metal Storehouses should be close to the mine mass they service.
- Normal non-opening Storehouses should improve weighted average haul by about >=5m for wood or >=4m for minerals; wood crisis may accept about >=2m.
- Reject zero-payback new-patch Storehouses. Farms/Farmsteads must never positively pull a Storehouse location toward them.

4) PERMANENT FOOD PROGRESS
- With natural food exhausted/near exhausted and desired Fields above the field pipeline, the AI must not sit at five Fields for minutes.
- If legal capacity exists and no Field pipeline progress occurs for ~24s, [EXPERT-FIELD-WATCH] should force one next Field.
- Natural-rich openings should still delay unnecessary permanent Fields normally.

5) FARMSTEAD #3
- Before a third farm_hub_deadlock Farmstead, look for [EXPERT-FARM-REPACK].
- If an old failed-slot mark was the only blocker and an existing hub has a legal slot, build that Field instead.
- If two hubs are truly boxed/full, Farmstead #3 must still be allowed; do not recreate a food-capacity deadlock.

6) ATHENS SCYTHIAN POLICE
- units/athen/city_police must not be trained as normal army replacement below the effective operating population cap.
- At the actual cap, zero-pop police may be added as overflow manpower.

7) HOUSE PACKING
- Prefer filling a legal one-house hole in an existing row/block before spreading, but never at the expense of Farm/Storehouse/resource reservations.

RECOMMENDED TEST MIX
- Athens Expert vs Athens Very Hard: watch contact fighting, police gating, healer/rams, and whether the army still walks past defenders to buildings.
- Random-civ Expert vs Very Hard on forest-heavy and sparse maps: inspect every non-opening Storehouse visually and correlate with wood-health/payback diagnostics.
- At least one food-poor replay: verify the five-Field plateau cannot persist while legal slots exist.
- Human vs Expert: judge whether attack-move pressure feels more coherent without creating suicidal chases or breaking tactical regroup.
