IT14.54 diagnostic replay (NON-BENCHMARK / ABORTED)
- Doctrine: P2 Forge-Tech Push. Normal 200-pop lobby.
- Regression was a permanent-food capacity deadlock, not a raw gather-rate problem: by ~6:23 Expert had five Farmsteads, only three Fields, natural food exhausted, and measured field capacity 3/0 while ten fields were wanted.
- By ~8:47 the deadlock had produced ~42 idle / 60 unproductive units with ~4253 wood banked; by ~12:47 it was ~53 idle / 57 unproductive with ~5314 wood and still Village/P2-waiting.
- Root cause: IT14.52 natural-food service created several dropsite Farmsteads, while the older permanent-farm anti-spam rule required >=4 network fields (or >=3 on one hub) before authorizing another hub. The network had zero legal field slots at three fields, so field #4 required a new Farmstead while the new Farmstead required field #4.
- Very Hard reached Town and gathered roughly 22.3k total resources versus Expert roughly 17.3k despite Expert's +35%; this run is rejected as a regression benchmark.
- The same review also found why the Village mining upgrades rarely appear: Servants and Wedge and Mallet are Village techs costing 200F/100S each, but the broad P1 eco sweep preserves the full Town-Phase reservation and the ordinary finite-resource mining gate waits for four completed fields. With no small pre-P2 stone bootstrap, the extra 100-200 stone for the mining pair often never exists; P2 core eco scheduling then deliberately buys food/wood first and has no dedicated mining lane.
