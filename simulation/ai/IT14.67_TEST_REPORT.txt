Expert AI Petra — IT14.67 validation / replay-response report
==============================================================

BASELINE
- Patched directly from exact IT14.66 source tree.
- Expert gather-rate multiplier remains 1.35.
- IT14.66 successful early-P1 timing gate thresholds are preserved.

WHY 14.67 EXISTS
Three IT14.66 tests produced one fail, one pass and one clear 16:58 win.
The winning replay still exposed the main remaining "pro" economy failure:
  6:55  ~141 food / 1004 wood
  7:43  ~74 food / 1991 wood
  8:47  ~66 food / 1844 wood
The controller already diagnosed wood>food, but could not convert that signal into action.
The first Town Market also failed placement repeatedly, leaving no barter outlet.
Replay 1 separately exposed runaway normal-Petra Champion production and idle Champions.
Replay 2 exposed an overly rigid 2/2 completed-tech launch gate.

IT14.67 CHANGES
1. Pro food-bank correction
   - Trigger: food < 250 and wood >= 1000.
   - Up to 6 healthy reserve Citizen Soldiers currently on wood become temporary food-owned workers.
   - If a field slot is open they farm; if not, the existing food-worker path makes them help finish
     fields/Farmsteads rather than continuing to inflate the wood bank.
   - They are released when food >= 500 or wood <= 750.
   - Diagnostic: [EXPERT-BANK] soldier wood->food / release temporary-food.

2. Emergency food barter
   - Once a Market exists and food < 250, sell disposable surplus to food.
   - Wood is strongly preferred when >= 1000, preserving a 700-wood reserve.
   - Stone/metal are secondary emergency sources above conservative floors.
   - Max transaction 500 with market-price feedback between trades.
   - Diagnostic: [EXPERT-BANK] barter buy=food ...

3. Utility-first Market placement
   - After the first normal Market placement failure, Market #1 may use the safe inner settlement ring
     down to 12m from the CC and a dense own-territory grid.
   - Resource corridors / decorative city-planning restrictions are relaxed in strategic fallback;
     engine obstruction, own-territory and same-access legality remain mandatory.

4. P2 Tech Push opportunity override
   - The normal 2 completed core-tech gate remains the baseline.
   - A 45+ army with >=1 active core tech can launch earlier ONLY if the existing timing-strength gate
     says the selected fight is favorable (including its local/global enemy-force test and exposed-economy retarget).
   - Diagnostic: [EXPERT-ATTACK] P2 opportunity override ...

5. Global AttackPlan Champion cap
   - Counts live Champions + AI queued plans + engine training.
   - Petra's normal AttackPlan path is capped at 6 non-hero Champions total, 4 ranged, 3 crossbows.
   - This closes the path that produced 17-21 champion crossbows ahead of rams.
   - Diagnostic: [EXPERT-CHAMPION] cap blocks attack-plan production ...

6. Premium-unit ownership correction
   - [EXPERT-PREMIUM] may now steal Champions assigned to stale/upcoming Petra plans when a real offensive
     is already active.
   - Champions belonging to another STARTED attack are preserved.

NON-REGRESSION INTENT
- Preserve the IT14.66 16:58 early-P1 win path.
- Do not mandate a Forge or Hoplite Tradition when a favorable P2 timing can already win.
- Do not globally weaken the P2 tech strategy; opportunity override still requires favorable combat math.
- Do not make Citizen Soldiers normal permanent farmers; the food assignment is an emergency lease.
- Do not convert all wood to food; preserve a 700-wood operating reserve.
